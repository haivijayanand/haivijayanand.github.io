28-Day Creo Parametric Course · Week 4 · Day 28

Capstone Project, Final Assessment and Certificate

Put it all together: a single-stage spur gear reducer from skeleton to parts, assembly, mechanism, drawings, BOM and a strength check. Then take the final assessment and print your certificate.

⏱ about 2–3 hours🧪 Lab: Exam + certificate🎓 Final assessment

1Today's goals

  • Plan a product with a skeleton and design intent
  • Model, assemble, animate, check and document it
  • Pass the 20-question final assessment
  • Know the next steps: PTC certification and beyond

2Capstone brief: spur gear reducer

Design a single-stage spur gear reducer: input 1450 rpm, ratio about 2.5 : 1, module 2, pinion 17 teeth, gear 42 teeth (centre distance 59 mm), face width 20. Bearings 6205 on the input, 6206 on the output. Cast housing split at the shaft plane with a bolted cover.

Part / itemSkills (day)
Skeleton: shaft axes, joint plane, bolt points, envelope; published LAYOUT8, 19
Housing (cast): split draft, shell, ribs, bearing bores, bosses4, 6, 11, 16
Cover: Copy Geometry from the skeleton19
Shafts: steps, grooves, keyways, cosmetic threads; parameters + relations5, 11, 12
Pinion and gear: one generic gear with a family table for tooth counts12, 13
Bolts and washers from family tables13
Assembly: Default, constraints, Pin connections, gear pair, servo, interference17, 18, 20
Reps, explode, sections, combined states20
Drawings with GD&T; GA with indented BOM and balloons22, 23, 24
Mass of the unit; output shaft stress check14, 27

3Deliverables checklist

  1. Working directory and config.pro set; all parts from the mmns templates with PART_NO, DESCRIPTION, MATERIAL parameters.
  2. Skeleton with A_IN, A_OUT (C = 59 as a named parameter), JOINT_FACE, bolt points; Publish Geometry LAYOUT.
  3. Housing with 2° split draft, 8 mm walls, bores Ø52 H7 and Ø62 H7, 6 × M8 tapped holes; cover from the same skeleton.
  4. Shafts with bearing seats (k6), circlip grooves, keyways; diameters driven by relations from one parameter.
  5. Gear generic with a family table (17T and 42T instances), pitch Ø = m·N by relation.
  6. Assembly: both shafts on Pin connections; gear pair 34 : 84; servo at 8700 deg/s; Position analysis played back with interference checking.
  7. Global Interference: only bearing press fits reported.
  8. View Manager: NO_FASTENERS rep, fitting-order explode, section A-A, combined state for the GA.
  9. Drawings: housing, cover, both shafts, gear; GA with indented BOM and balloons; title blocks from parameters; first-angle.
  10. Mass of the whole unit; output-shaft bending stress by hand and in Creo Simulate.

4Final assessment

Twenty questions from all four weeks. 70 % is the pass mark.

5Certificate

Type your name. The certificate shows how many days you have marked complete and your best final-assessment score. Print it or save as PDF.

6What next

  • PTC University learning paths and the Creo certification exams (associate and professional levels) — check PTC's site for the current programme.
  • Go deeper where your work is: Pro/PROGRAM and Creo TOOLKIT for automation, Creo Simulate Advanced, Mechanism Dynamics, Advanced Assembly Extension, Piping and Cabling.
  • Re-model a real part from your own work every week. Speed comes from repetition; design intent comes from being made to change your models.
