2IRR traps and real options

Multiple IRRs: cash flows that change sign more than once (e.g., a mine with clean-up costs at the end) can have several IRRs or none; use NPV or modified IRR. Scale and timing: IRR can prefer a small high-return project over a larger one that adds more value. Real options: the right to delay, expand or abandon has value that static NPV misses (Dixit & Pindyck 1994).
