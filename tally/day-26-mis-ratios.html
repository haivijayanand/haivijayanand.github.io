MIS Reports and Ratio Analysis

Management information from TallyPrime: Day Book, registers, cash and funds flow, receivables and payables, stock and movement analysis, exception reports — and ratio analysis that turns the statements into a health check.

⏱ about 2 hours🧪 Lab: Ratio dashboard❓ 10-question quiz

1Today's goals

  • Know which report answers which management question
  • Read cash flow and funds flow
  • Use exception reports to catch errors
  • Calculate and interpret key ratios
  • Build a monthly MIS pack from TallyPrime

2Which report answers which question

QuestionReport (Alt+G → …)
What happened today?Day Book
How much did we sell / buy each month?Sales Register, Purchase Register
Where did the cash come from and go?Cash Flow, Funds Flow
Who owes us / whom do we owe, and how old?Outstandings, Ageing Analysis
Which items sell, to whom, at what margin?Movement Analysis, Stock Item report, Item-wise profit
Is anything odd?Exception reports: negative stock, negative ledgers, overdue bills, memorandum and optional vouchers, post-dated vouchers
Are we healthy?Ratio Analysis

4A monthly MIS pack

  1. Profit & Loss for the month and year-to-date with last year's column.
  2. Balance Sheet; Ratio Analysis.
  3. Receivables ageing and top overdue bills; payables due next month.
  4. Stock Summary by group; slow-moving items (Movement Analysis).
  5. Budget variance by cost centre.
  6. Export each with Alt+E to Excel or PDF, or share with Alt+M.
