GD&T Basics: Symbols, Datums, Feature Control Frames, MMC and Bonus Tolerance

K Vijay Anand · Vijay_Wiki quick-reference article · 24 November 2026
GD&TASME Y14.5ISO 1101Drawing

Plus-minus dimensions control size well but describe form, orientation and location poorly: a ±0.1 mm square zone for a hole position is both too strict at the corners and ambiguous about where measurement starts. Geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (ASME Y14.5, ISO GPS/ISO 1101) defines tolerance zones relative to datums, so the drawing states exactly what the function needs.

Key takeaways

  • Four control types: form (no datum), orientation, location and runout (all need datums); profile can be either.
  • Datums A, B, C build a reference frame (3-2-1 contact): primary constrains three degrees of freedom, secondary two, tertiary one.
  • A diametral position zone (⌀) has 57 % more area than the equivalent ± square zone and matches how round parts fit.
  • At MMC (Ⓜ) the tolerance grows by the amount the feature departs from its maximum material size: bonus tolerance.
  • Fastener formulas: floating T = H − F; fixed T = (H − F)/2, with H the hole MMC and F the fastener MMC.

2. Reading a feature control frame

Read it as: "The axis of this hole must lie within a cylindrical zone ⌀0.2 at maximum material condition, located at its basic (theoretically exact, boxed) dimensions from datums A, then B, then C."

ModifierMeaning
Ⓜ MMCMaximum material condition: smallest hole / largest pin. Bonus tolerance as the feature departs from MMC.
Ⓛ LMCLeast material condition: protects minimum wall thickness.
(none) RFSRegardless of feature size: no bonus (default in ASME Y14.5-2009 onward).
ⓅProjected tolerance zone (threaded holes for studs)
ⒻFree state (flexible parts)
CZCombined zone across several features

3. Datums

Datum features are real surfaces; datums are the perfect planes, axes or points simulated from them by inspection equipment. Choose datums that match how the part is located in assembly: the mounting face as A, a locating bore or edge as B, an anti-rotation feature as C.
