    Ledger vs cost centre

    A ledger says what the money was spent on (Wages, Power). A cost centre says where or for whom (Machining, Assembly). One wages payment of ₹1,86,000 can be split 55 % Machining, 10 % Heat Treatment, 35 % Assembly — and then Avani knows what each department really costs.

    Cost categories are parallel sets of cost centres: for example Departments (Machining, Assembly…) and Products (GB-20, SG-40). The same expense can be allocated in both.

    GST in one paragraph

    GST is a tax on each supply of goods or services. Avani collects GST on what it sells (output tax), pays GST on what it buys (input tax), and pays the government only the difference — input tax credit (ITC) makes sure tax is paid once, on the value added at each stage. The final consumer bears it.

    Which GST: CGST + SGST or IGST?

    Supplier's state vs place of supplyTaxExample at 18 %
    Same state (intra-state)CGST + SGST, half eachAvani (TN) → Kaveri Pumps (TN): CGST 9 % + SGST 9 %
    Different states (inter-state)IGST, full rateAvani (TN) → Deccan Tractors (Telangana): IGST 18 %

    For goods that move, the place of supply is generally where the movement ends — the customer's location.

    Rates, codes and registration

    • Rates: from 22 September 2025 GST uses two main rates, 5 % and 18 %, a special 40 % for luxury and "sin" goods, plus nil-rated and exempt goods. Avani's gearboxes (HSN 8483), bearings (8482) and alloy-steel bars (7228) are at 18 %. Always confirm the current rate for each HSN.
    • HSN codes classify goods, SAC codes classify services (job work, freight, audit).
    • GSTIN: 15 characters — state code (2), PAN (10), entity number (1), "Z" (1), check character (1).

    Reverse charge (RCM)

    Normally the supplier charges and pays the GST. For some supplies the law makes the recipient pay it instead — for example freight from a goods transport agency that does not charge GST, or legal services from an advocate. Avani then pays the GST in cash and can take the same amount as input credit.

    Set-off of input tax credit

    ITC is used in a fixed order: IGST credit first against IGST, then against CGST and SGST; CGST credit against CGST, then IGST; SGST credit against SGST, then IGST. CGST can never pay SGST, nor SGST pay CGST. Whatever is left is paid in cash.

    Reverse charge, e-invoice, e-way bill

    • RCM entry: book the expense (for example GTA freight ₹20,000) normally; TallyPrime raises the RCM liability (5 % for GTA in this example) in the GST reports when the ledger is marked "reverse charge applicable"; pay it in cash; claim it as ITC in the same return.
    • E-invoice: B2B invoices must be registered on the Invoice Registration Portal (IRN and QR code) once aggregate turnover in any year since 2017-18 crosses ₹5 crore. Avani's ₹2.74 crore turnover is below it today — but the day it crosses, TallyPrime can generate the IRN from the invoice itself.
    • E-way bill: needed to move goods of value above ₹50,000 in one consignment (and for some job-work movements whatever the value). TallyPrime generates it online from the invoice or delivery note with vehicle details.

    TDS in plain words

    When Avani pays certain amounts — job work, contract labour, professional and technical fees, rent — it must deduct income tax at source, pay the party the rest, and deposit the deducted tax with the government. The party gets credit for it against their own income tax.

    New law, same rates. The Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the 1961 Act on 1 April 2026. The old sections (194C, 194J…) are now consolidated in section 393 (salary TDS in section 392, TCS in section 394); rates and limits stayed broadly the same. Payments or credits from 1 April 2026 must quote the new provisions. Use a TallyPrime release that supports the new Act.
