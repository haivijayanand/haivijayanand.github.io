Journal Voucher (F7): Adjustments and Non-cash Entries

The Journal voucher (F7) is for everything that is neither cash nor bank: credit expenses, provisions, depreciation, prepaid and outstanding items, interest charged to a loan, and corrections of earlier mistakes.

⏱ about 2 hours🧪 Lab: Practice Desk: journals❓ 10-question quiz

1Today's goals

  • Know when to use Journal instead of Payment or Receipt
  • Book an expense on credit and a provision
  • Book depreciation and a prepaid adjustment
  • Correct a wrong posting with a rectification entry
  • Enter seven adjustment entries in the Practice Desk

2When to use Journal

Journal (F7) — any entry where neither side is cash or bank. By default TallyPrime does not show cash and bank ledgers in a journal (you can allow them with F12), which keeps payments and receipts in their own vouchers and cash books clean.
SituationEntry
Expense bill received, to be paid laterDr expense · Cr supplier
Provision (expense belongs to this year, bill not yet received)Dr expense · Cr provision (e.g. Audit Fees Payable)
DepreciationDr Depreciation · Cr the asset
Prepaid expense (paid now, belongs to later months)Dr Prepaid · Cr the expense
Interest charged to a loan accountDr Interest · Cr the loan
Correction (posted to the wrong ledger)Dr correct ledger · Cr wrong ledger

The principle behind provisions, prepaid and outstanding entries is accrual: an expense belongs to the period in which it is used, whatever the date it is paid. Without it, monthly profit swings with the payment calendar.

3Practice Desk: journals

4Now in TallyPrime

  1. Vouchers → F7. The first line is By (Dr) by default; type the ledger and amount; the next line offers To (Cr) with the balance pre-filled.
  2. Enter the seven journals. Then open Alt+G → Profit & Loss A/c and find Depreciation, Audit Fees and Insurance under Indirect Expenses.
  3. Open the Balance Sheet: Audit Fees Payable and Salary Payable sit under Current Liabilities, Prepaid Insurance under Current Assets.
