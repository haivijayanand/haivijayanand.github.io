How the Mini Crossword works

Fill every white square so that each numbered word reads correctly Across (left to right) or Down (top to bottom). Where words cross, they share a letter, so solving one word gives you letters in another.

How each puzzle is made

  1. Pick the words from the same clue bank as Clue Guess: one theme, or a mix of all themes in your topic.
  2. Lay down the first word across the middle of an empty grid. A long word is chosen, because it gives the most letters for others to cross.
  3. Find every crossing. For each remaining word, the generator looks at every letter already on the grid that the word also contains, and tries running the word through it in the other direction.
  4. Apply the crossword rules. A position is allowed only if every shared square has the same letter, no word touches another side-by-side except where they cross, and no word runs straight into another end to end. These rules ensure every run of two or more letters on the grid is a real clued word.
  5. Prefer more crossings. Among the allowed positions, the one that crosses the most existing words is chosen, which makes a tighter puzzle.
  6. Keep the best of many tries. The whole layout is built up to 60 times with the words in different orders, and the grid with the most words (then the most crossings) wins. The empty border is then trimmed.
  7. Give some letters away according to the level, never a whole word.
