28-Day Creo Parametric Course

From the working directory and your first sketch to relations, family tables, assemblies, mechanisms, skeletons, sheetmetal, drawings, surfacing and Creo Simulate. Every day has a clear goal, step-by-step work to do in Creo, an interactive lab, pro tips, a practice part and a quiz. Day 28 ends with a capstone project, a final assessment and a certificate. Coming from SolidWorks? Start with the SolidWorks → Creo command map.

28 days · ≈ 2–3 h each28 interactive labs135 quiz questions + final examworks offline once loaded
How each day works1 Read the idea · 2 Do the steps in Creo (click a step to tick it) · 3 Play with the lab · 4 Read the tips · 5 Make the practice part · 6 Take the quiz · 7 Mark the day complete.
What you needCreo Parametric 12 (menu paths are the same back to Creo 7 for almost everything; Creo 4–6 differ a little). Mechanism, Simulate and Flexible Modeling are in most packages; the GD&T Advisor and advanced surfacing are extensions.
KeyboardM contents · F full screen · T light/dark · P play · S step · X pause · [ ] previous / next day. In 3D labs: drag to rotate, wheel to zoom, double-click to reset.
Units and standardsMillimetres (mmns templates), ISO standards and first-angle projection as used in India — third angle is covered too.

Coming from SolidWorks?

Creo thinks differently about sketches, references and history. The SolidWorks → Creo command map lists 70+ commands side by side, with the traps that catch SolidWorks users. The SolidWorks course is on this site too.
