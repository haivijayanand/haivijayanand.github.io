Flexible Laminate Structures: Layers, Grammage, Yield and Barrier (OTR, WVTR)

K Vijay Anand · Vijay_Wiki quick-reference article · 24 November 2026
LaminatesBarrierOTRWVTR

A flexible laminate combines films so that each does one job: an outer film for printing, stiffness and heat resistance; a barrier layer against oxygen, moisture and light; and an inner sealant that makes the pack. Understanding grammage, yield and barrier arithmetic lets you compare structures and costs quickly.

Key takeaways

  • Grammage (g/m²) = thickness (µm) × density (g/cm³); add 2–3 g/m² per adhesive layer and 1–2 g/m² for ink.
  • Yield (m²/kg) = 1,000 / total grammage. Cost per m² = Σ(layer grammage × price per kg)/1,000.
  • Barrier layers in series add resistances: 1/OTRtotal = Σ 1/OTRi (same for WVTR).
  • Metallised films cut OTR and WVTR by 10–100× compared with plain films; aluminium foil is near-total barrier when pinhole-free.
  • Transmission rates depend strongly on temperature and humidity: always quote test conditions (e.g. 23 °C, 0 % RH for OTR; 38 °C, 90 % RH for WVTR).
