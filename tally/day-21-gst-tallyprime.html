GST in TallyPrime: Setup and GST Invoices

GST in TallyPrime: enable GST and enter Avani's registration, create tax ledgers, set HSN and rate on stock items, give parties their GSTIN and state — then raise intra-state and inter-state GST invoices and a GST credit note.

⏱ about 2 hours🧪 Lab: Practice Desk: GST invoices❓ 10-question quiz

1Today's goals

  • Enable GST and set company GST details
  • Create Output and Input CGST, SGST and IGST ledgers
  • Set GST on items and ledgers
  • Raise GST purchase and sales invoices
  • Reverse GST in a credit note

2Setting up GST

  1. F11 → Taxation → Enable Goods and Services Tax (GST) = Yes → set GST details: state Tamil Nadu, registration type Regular, GSTIN 33AAKCA4821M1Z6, periodicity of GSTR-1 Monthly.
  2. Tax ledgers under Duties & Taxes, type of duty GST: Output CGST / Input CGST (Central Tax), Output SGST / Input SGST (State Tax), Output IGST / Input IGST (Integrated Tax). Leave the percentage blank so the rate comes from the item.
  3. Sales and purchase ledgers: Sales - GST 18%, Sales - Interstate 18%, Purchase - GST 18%, Purchase - Interstate 18% with GST applicable, type of supply Goods.
  4. Stock items: HSN and rate (Day 13). Parties: state, registration type Regular, GSTIN.

In an invoice, after the items, select the tax ledgers: TallyPrime calculates the tax from each item's rate and the place of supply. If you pick CGST/SGST for an inter-state party, it warns you.

Many companies use one sales ledger and let TallyPrime decide the tax type; the separate local and inter-state ledgers here make the Sales Register easier to read while you learn.

3Practice Desk: GST invoices

Enter the tax lines yourself — taxable value × rate, split in two for intra-state.

4Now in TallyPrime

  1. Complete the setup above and enter the six invoices.
  2. On each invoice press Alt+A (Tax Analysis) to see the GST by rate.
  3. Open Alt+G → GSTR-1: B2B invoices listed by customer; and GSTR-3B: output tax, ITC and net liability.
