SolidWorks Course Part II · Week 6 · Day 41

Rendering, Visualize and eDrawings

A good picture sells a design review. Appearances, decals, scenes and cameras in SolidWorks, photorealistic rendering in SOLIDWORKS Visualize, and eDrawings for sharing.

⏱ about 2–3 hours🧪 Lab: Lighting lab❓ 5-question quiz

1Today's goals

  • Apply appearances, decals and scenes
  • Set up cameras and lighting
  • Render in SOLIDWORKS Visualize
  • Share with eDrawings and images

2From shaded model to render

In SolidWorks itself: Appearances (Task Pane ▸ Appearances, Scenes and Decals) on faces, features, bodies or parts; Decals for labels and logos; Scenes (environment and floor); RealView graphics for realistic display; Cameras (View ▸ Lights and Cameras ▸ Add Camera).

For photorealistic images and animations, SOLIDWORKS Visualize is the tool: it is a separate program that uses the GPU, and Visualize Standard is included with SOLIDWORKS Professional and Premium on subscription. PhotoView 360 was retired from SOLIDWORKS 2024 onwards. Import the SolidWorks assembly (keeping appearances), choose an environment (HDR), place cameras, render.

Key lightMain light, 30–45° up and to one side.
FillSofter light or the environment, opening the shadows.
Rim / back lightSeparates the part from the background.
CameraLonger focal length (50–85 mm) for less distortion; slight downward view.

For sharing: eDrawings files (.eprt, .easm, .edrw) and the free eDrawings viewer let reviewers rotate, measure and mark up without SolidWorks.

3Do it

  1. Apply appearances: housing cast iron painted, shafts machined steel, gears case-hardened steel.
  2. Add a decal with your company logo and part number on the cover.
  3. Apply a scene (Studio) and save a camera at a three-quarter view.
  4. Open SOLIDWORKS Visualize, import the assembly (Grouping: Appearance). Choose an HDR environment.
  5. Render a 1920 × 1080 image; then an exploded-view turntable animation (Day 20 explode imported).
  6. Save the assembly as eDrawings (.easm) with the exploded view; send it for review.

4Lab: light the part

Move the key light and change fill to see how lighting reveals or hides the shape of a pulley. Drag to rotate.

5Pro tips and common mistakes

  • Apply appearances at the part level for parts; assembly-level appearances override them only in that assembly.
  • Dark materials need more light; a black part on a black background disappears.
  • Use a plain background and consistent camera for every image in a report.
  • Render resolution: about 2× the size you will print or display, no more.

6Practice

Make a one-page product sheet for the reducer: hero render, exploded render, three key specifications and a QR code to the eDrawings file on your intranet.
