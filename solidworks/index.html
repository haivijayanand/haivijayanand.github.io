Free · interactive · Part I 28 days + Part II 14 days

SolidWorks Course

From your first sketch to drawings, assemblies, sheet metal, weldments, surfaces and simulation. Every day has a clear goal, step-by-step work to do in SolidWorks, an interactive lab — most of them in 3D — pro tips, a practice part and a quiz. Day 28 ends with a capstone project, a final assessment and a certificate. Part II adds 14 days on automation and documentation, including using an AI assistant safely for macros, change notices, reports, test reports and RFQs.

42 days · ≈ 2–3 h each42 interactive labs200 quiz questions + two final examsworks offline once loaded
How each day works1 Read the idea · 2 Do the steps in SolidWorks (click a step to tick it) · 3 Play with the lab · 4 Read the tips · 5 Make the practice part · 6 Take the quiz · 7 Mark the day complete.
What you needSolidWorks 2020 or newer (any edition for Weeks 1–2 and most of 3–4). Toolbox needs Professional or Premium; Motion Analysis and full Simulation need Premium or Simulation licences — SimulationXpress is in every edition.
KeyboardM contents · F full screen · T light/dark · P play · S step · X pause · [ ] previous / next day. In 3D labs: drag to rotate, wheel to zoom, double-click to reset.
Units and standardsMillimetres (MMGS), ISO standards and first-angle projection as used in India — third angle is covered too. Menu paths follow recent SolidWorks releases; older ones may differ slightly.
