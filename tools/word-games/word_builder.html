How Word Builder works

You get a set of letter tiles. Make as many real English words as you can, using each tile at most once per word. One word always uses every tile; finding it earns a big bonus.

Where the words come from

  1. Two dictionaries. English words are ranked by how often they appear in everyday writing. Kids mode uses about 8,200 of the most common words. Adult mode adds about 14,500 less common ones, for about 22,700 in total.
  2. Cleaned up. Every word must also appear in two independent English word lists, which removes names, abbreviations and internet slang. Offensive words and filler sounds such as "hmm" are removed.
  3. Science words. About 320 science terms (planets, elements, body parts, physics and chemistry words) are marked as science words.

How each puzzle is made

  1. Pick a seed word with exactly as many letters as the level has tiles. English topic picks a common word; Science topic picks a science word; All picks either.
  2. Check it in advance. Each seed word was tested when the game was built: it must give a sensible number of findable words for its level, not too few to be boring and not too many to finish.
  3. Find every answer. The game scans the whole dictionary and keeps each word whose letters fit inside the tiles. A word fits if, for every letter, it needs no more copies than the tiles have.
  4. Shuffle the tiles so the seed word isn't shown in order.

Ranks

Your rank is your score as a share of the most points possible in this puzzle: Beginner, then Good (15%), Great (30%), Amazing (50%), Genius (70%), and Champion when every word is found.

With the timer on, the game ends when time runs out. Press Finish at any time to end the game and see the words you missed. Your best score is kept in this browser for each combination of mode, topic, level and timer.
