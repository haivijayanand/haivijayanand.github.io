Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors: A 10-Day Course

Some directions are special: a matrix only stretches them, never turns them. Those directions — and the stretch factors that go with them — are why a beam fails along one plane, why a bridge has a handful of dangerous frequencies, why an aircraft porpoises, why a market settles to fixed shares, how Google ranks pages and how a JPEG-like image shrinks to a few percent. Ten days, each with the idea, worked examples with real numbers, live labs you can drag and play, and a quiz.
