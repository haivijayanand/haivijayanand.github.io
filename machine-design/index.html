30-Day Machine Design Course

Design of machine elements from first principles to a complete gearbox. Each day takes one family of elements and works through how it fails, the design criteria and formulas, the materials that suit it, what drives its cost, a worked example, a step-by-step design procedure, a live calculator or plot to experiment with, a practice problem and a quiz. Day 30 ends with a two-stage gearbox capstone, a final assessment and a certificate.

30 days · ≈ 2–3 h each30 interactive labs145 quiz questions + 20-question final examSI units · IS / ISO standards
How each day works1 Read how the element works and fails · 2 Learn the design criteria and formulas · 3 Choose materials and see the cost drivers · 4 Follow the worked example · 5 Play with the lab · 6 Solve the practice problem · 7 Take the quiz and mark the day complete.
What you needStrength of materials and engineering mechanics at diploma or B.E. level, a calculator, and a design data book (PSG Design Data or V B Bhandari's tables are the usual ones in India). Shigley, Bhandari and Norton are the textbooks the methods follow.

Units and standardsSI throughout: N, mm, MPa (N/mm²), kW, rpm. Indian (IS) and ISO standards are named where they apply; AGMA and ASME are mentioned where they are the common reference.
