Advanced and Mechanical Mates

Beyond flat-on-flat: advanced mates limit and centre, mechanical mates make gears turn, cams follow and screws advance.

⏱ about 2–3 hours🧪 Lab: Gear mate animation❓ 5-question quiz

1Today's goals

  • Use Profile Center, Symmetric, Width, Path and Limit mates
  • Use Gear, Rack Pinion, Screw, Cam, Slot, Hinge and Universal Joint mates
  • Set gear ratios correctly
  • Test motion by dragging

2Advanced mates

Mechanical mates move parts kinematically; they do not check tooth contact. Gears in a gear mate may pass through each other if they are not first aligned — rotate one gear to mesh before adding the mate.

3Do it in SolidWorks: gear pair

  1. Make two simple gears (or use Toolbox ▸ ISO ▸ Power Transmission ▸ Gears ▸ Spur Gear): module 2, 17 and 42 teeth, face width 20, bores Ø15 and Ø25.
  2. Assembly: a plate with two shafts 59 mm apart (a = m(N1+N2)/2 = 2 × 59 / 2 = 59).
  3. Concentric + Coincident each gear on its shaft (leave rotation free).
  4. Rotate one gear until the teeth mesh visually (Rotate Component).
  5. Mate ▸ Mechanical Mates ▸ Gear: select both bore faces; Ratio 34 mm : 84 mm (pitch diameters).
  6. Drag the pinion: the gear turns 17/42 as fast, in the opposite direction.
  7. Add a Screw mate to the vice from Day 16 (lead 4 mm/rev) and a Limit Distance 0–60 on the jaw.

4Lab: gear mate

Change tooth counts and module; the centre distance and ratio are computed for you. Press P to run, S to step one pinion tooth at a time — watch how many teeth the gear advances.

5Pro tips and common mistakes

  • Gear ratio in SolidWorks is entered as the pitch diameters, so check the direction — tick Reverse for an internal gear or for a belt/pulley where both turn the same way.
  • Limit mates can make solving slower; use them only where travel really matters.
  • For a cam, the cam faces must form a tangent-continuous loop (extrude a closed sketch of tangent arcs/splines).
  • To check real contact and forces, use a Motion Analysis study (Day 20) with contact — not mates alone.

6Practice

Build a rack and pinion: pinion module 2, 20 teeth; rack length 200. Rack Pinion mate with pinion pitch Ø 40 → rack travels π × 40 ≈ 125.7 mm per pinion revolution. Add Limit Distance so the rack cannot run off the pinion.
