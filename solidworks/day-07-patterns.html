28-Day SolidWorks Course · Week 1 · Day 07

Patterns and Mirror

Make one feature and let SolidWorks copy it: linear, circular, curve- and sketch-driven patterns, and mirror. Patterns carry intent such as "always 6 equally spaced".

⏱ about 2–3 hours🧪 Lab: Pattern builder❓ 5-question quiz

1Today's goals

  • Create linear and circular patterns, skip instances
  • Use Equal spacing and Up To Reference
  • Know sketch-, curve-, table-driven and fill patterns
  • Mirror features and bodies about a plane or face

2One seed, many copies

PatternDriven byTypical use
Linear PatternOne or two directions (edges, axes, dimensions); spacing + instances, or Up To ReferenceHole grids, cooling fins
Circular PatternAxis (temporary axis, edge or axis feature); angle + instances; Equal spacingFlange bolt holes, spokes
Curve Driven PatternA sketch curve or edgeHoles along an arc or spline
Sketch Driven PatternPoints in a sketchIrregular hole positions
Table Driven PatternX–Y table (or .sldptab / text file) relative to a coordinate systemPCB-style coordinates
Fill PatternFills a face boundary with a layoutPerforated panels, vent grilles
Variable PatternInstances with changing dimensions (table)Fins of increasing height
MirrorA plane or planar faceSymmetric features or whole bodies

Instances to Skip removes chosen copies without breaking the pattern. Geometry pattern copies only faces (faster, but ignores end conditions). Turn on View ▸ Hide/Show ▸ Temporary Axes to use the hidden axis of any cylindrical face for a circular pattern.

3Do it in SolidWorks: flange

  1. Revolve a flange: OD 200, thickness 20, hub Ø90 × 30 high, bore Ø50.
  2. Sketch one Ø18 hole on the flange face at PCD 160 (construction circle Ø160, hole centre on it, vertical relation to the origin) → Extruded Cut Through All.
  3. Features ▸ Linear Pattern ▸ Circular Pattern: axis = temporary axis of the bore, Equal spacing, 360°, 8 instances.
  4. Edit the pattern: 6 instances. Then skip one instance (click its pink dot in Instances to Skip).
  5. Plate exercise: a 300 × 180 plate with a Ø14 hole at (25, 25) → Linear Pattern: Direction 1 along the long edge, 50 mm × 6; Direction 2 along the short edge, 50 mm × 3.
  6. Mirror: model a slot on one side of a symmetric bracket and Mirror it about the Right Plane.

4Lab: patterns

Linear mode: click instances to skip them, and watch for instances that fall off the plate. Circular mode: switch Equal spacing on and off with less than 360°. Mirror mode: the copy follows the seed.

5Pro tips and common mistakes

  • Pattern the feature, not the sketch: patterned features rebuild faster than a sketch with 40 circles.
  • Equal spacing with 360° divides by N; with a smaller angle it divides the angle by N − 1 (first and last instance at the ends).
  • Pattern direction can be a dimension — then spacing follows that dimension's direction.
  • Mirror whole bodies for left/right-hand parts, and use Insert ▸ Mirror Part to create a linked opposite-hand part file.
  • Hole Wizard holes in a pattern still carry callout information to drawings — better than plain cuts.

6Practice: vented cover

Plate 200 × 150 × 3 with R10 corners. Make one 4 × 30 slot and pattern it 12 × 2 with 12 mm spacing; skip the four corner instances. Add 4 × Ø6 holes on a 180 × 130 rectangle using one sketch-driven pattern. Mirror any feature that is only on one side.
