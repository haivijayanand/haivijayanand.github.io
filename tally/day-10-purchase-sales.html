Purchase (F9) and Sales (F8) in Accounting Mode

Purchase (F9) and Sales (F8) vouchers for goods bought and sold, first without inventory and GST so the accounting is clear: credit and cash purchases and sales, the purchase and sales ledgers, and the three voucher modes.

⏱ about 2 hours🧪 Lab: Practice Desk: purchase and sales❓ 10-question quiz

1Today's goals

  • Record credit and cash purchases
  • Record credit and cash sales
  • Know the three modes: Item Invoice, Accounting Invoice, As Voucher
  • Read the Purchase and Sales Registers
  • Enter six Avani purchase and sales entries

2Purchase and Sales

Use Purchase (F9) whenever goods for the business (raw material, components, goods for resale) are bought — for cash or on credit. Use Sales (F8) whenever goods are sold. They feed the Purchase and Sales Registers, the GST returns and (with inventory) the stock records.

EntryDrCr
Credit purchase of steelPurchase AccountSupplier
Cash purchase of gear oilPurchase AccountCash
Credit sale of gearboxesCustomerSales Account
Cash saleCashSales Account
Buying a machine is not a "purchase" in this sense — it is a fixed asset. Record it with a Journal (credit) or Payment (cash), or in a Purchase voucher using the asset ledger, never in Purchase Account; otherwise the machine's cost would reduce this year's profit.
