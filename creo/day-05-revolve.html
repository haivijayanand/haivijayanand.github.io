Revolve and Turned Parts

Turned parts — shafts, bushes, pulleys, flanges — are revolves. Learn the revolve axis, angle options, how to sketch half a section, and how to check volume with Pappus.

⏱ about 2–3 hours🧪 Lab: 3D revolve + Pappus❓ 5-question quiz

1Today's goals

  • Sketch a half-section with a geometry centreline as the axis
  • Use Variable, Symmetric and To Selected angles
  • Make revolved cuts: grooves, undercuts, chamfers in the sketch
  • Know when to revolve and when to extrude
  • Check volume with Pappus' theorem

2Revolve basics

Model ▸ Revolve spins a sketch about an axis. The axis is either a geometry centreline in the sketch (preferred — it lives with the sketch) or an existing datum axis or edge picked in the Placement panel.

Sketch halfDraw only half the section, on one side of the axis, closed along the axis.
Diameter dimensionsDimension a point to the centreline, then pick the centreline again and the point again: Creo shows a diameter. Shops read diameters, not radii.
AngleVariable (typed, default 360°), Symmetric, or To Selected.
Remove MaterialRevolved cuts make grooves, undercuts and inside profiles.
Pappus' theorem: volume of a revolved area = area × distance travelled by its centroid = A × 2π·r̄. A quick hand check of mass for any turned part.

3Do it in Creo

  1. New part shaft_d5. Model ▸ Revolve → Define → sketch on FRONT.
  2. Draw a Geometry Centerline (not a construction centreline) along the horizontal TOP reference.
  3. Sketch the stepped half profile above the centreline: Ø30 × 40, Ø40 × 60, Ø25 × 50, closed along the axis.
  4. Dimension diameters (point – centreline – point). Add a 1 × 45° chamfer in the sketch at each end using Chamfer (sketch).
  5. Angle 360°. Finish. Name it SHAFT_BODY.
  6. Revolve again with Remove Material: sketch a 2 × 1.1 groove rectangle on the Ø25 step 5 from its end (a circlip groove), axis = the centreline in that sketch. Finish.
  7. Make a datum axis through the shaft (Model ▸ Axis, pick the cylindrical face). You will use it as a reference for holes and assemblies.
  8. Analysis ▸ Mass Properties → compare the volume with your Pappus estimate.

4Lab: revolve in 3D + Pappus

Change the profile and the angle and compare the computed volume with the Pappus formula.

5Pro tips and common mistakes

  • "Section must be on one side of the axis": a line crosses the centreline. Keep the profile on one side, closed along the axis.
  • A sketch with two geometry centrelines: Creo uses the first one as the axis — check it in Placement.
  • Put the turned part's axis on a default datum (TOP/FRONT intersection). Assemblies then use the datum axis, not a random surface.
  • Model chamfers and small radii as separate features if they might change; in the sketch if they are permanent.
  • Pulleys with spokes: revolve the rim and hub, extrude one spoke, then pattern it (Day 7).

6Practice

  1. Model a flanged bush: Ø60 × 10 flange, Ø40 × 30 body, Ø25 H7 bore, 1 × 45° chamfers, one revolved feature plus one revolved cut.
  2. Make a V-pulley: Ø100 outside, one 38° V-groove 11 deep, Ø20 bore, hub Ø40 × 30.
  3. Check both volumes with Pappus and with Mass Properties.
