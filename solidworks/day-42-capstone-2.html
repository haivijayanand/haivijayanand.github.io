SolidWorks Course Part II · Week 6 · Day 42

Part II Capstone, Assessment and Certificate

Put Part II together: a documentation and automation pack for the gear reducer — macros, configurator, controlled revisions, stack-up, cost, reports, RFQs and renders — then the Part II assessment and certificate.

⏱ about 2–3 hours🧪 Lab: Exam + certificate🎓 Final assessment

1Today's goals

  • Deliver a complete documentation pack for one product
  • Use automation and AI where they save time, and checks where they add risk
  • Pass the 15-question Part II assessment
  • Plan your next steps

2Capstone brief

Take the Part I gear reducer (Day 28) and produce the pack a real project would hand over.

DeliverableSkills (day)
Reviewed macro that exports all drawings to PDF and parts to STEP29, 30
DriveWorksXpress configurator for the shafts; Design Checker standard31
All files in PDM (or a controlled folder) at revision A, then a change to B with ECN32, 33
Supplier bearing and motor STEP imported, repaired, defeatured34, 35
Axial stack-up of the output shaft, worst case and RSS36
Cost estimate at 25 and 250 sets37
Design report (analysis section) with a fact list and number check38
Test report results section from a sample run log39
Three RFQs and a quote comparison sheet40
Hero render, exploded render and eDrawings file41

3Deliverables checklist

  1. Macro folder with header blocks and a "reviewed by / date" note.
  2. Configurator generates four shaft variants with correct names and drawings.
  3. Revision B released with revision table entries and ECN.
  4. Imported supplier parts with mate references in your library.
  5. Stack-up sheet with the tolerance you chose to tighten and why.
  6. Cost table with assumptions and rates.
  7. Design report section: fact list, AI draft, number-check record, final text.
  8. Test report results: data summary, plot, final text, raw file list.
  9. RFQs sent (or ready) and comparison sheet.
  10. Renders and eDrawings in the release folder; everything packed with Pack and Go.

4Part II assessment

Fifteen questions across Days 29–41. 70 % to pass.

5Part II certificate

Shows how many of Days 29–42 you have marked complete and your best Part II score.

6What next

  • Certifications: CSWP, then specialist exams such as CSWPA-Drawing Tools, Sheet Metal, Weldments and Simulation (CSWA-S).
  • Learn the SolidWorks API properly (C# add-ins) if macros become part of your daily work.
  • Write your team's rules: macro review, AI-tool use, revision scheme, report fact lists. Rules make the speed safe.
