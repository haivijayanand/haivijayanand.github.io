Kaveri Foods' product lines

Generic strategies (Porter): compete on cost leadership, differentiation, or focus on a niche (cost or differentiation). Being "stuck in the middle" — neither cheapest nor distinct — tends to earn the lowest returns.

1VRIO: is the advantage lasting?

Test a resource

Barney (1991): sustained advantage comes from resources that are valuable, rare, inimitable and non-substitutable; VRIO adds whether the organisation can exploit them. Dynamic capabilities (Teece, Pisano & Shuen 1997) stress the ability to reconfigure resources as markets change.
