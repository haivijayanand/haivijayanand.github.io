TDS and TCS in TallyPrime

Tax Deducted at Source in TallyPrime under the Income-tax Act, 2025 (in force from 1 April 2026): who must deduct, the main rates and limits for contractors and professionals, booking a bill with TDS, depositing it, and where TCS still applies.

2TDS in plain words

When Avani pays certain amounts — job work, contract labour, professional and technical fees, rent — it must deduct income tax at source, pay the party the rest, and deposit the deducted tax with the government. The party gets credit for it against their own income tax.

New law, same rates. The Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the 1961 Act on 1 April 2026. The old sections (194C, 194J…) are now consolidated in section 393 (salary TDS in section 392, TCS in section 394); rates and limits stayed broadly the same. Payments or credits from 1 April 2026 must quote the new provisions. Use a TallyPrime release that supports the new Act.

No PAN → a higher rate (generally 20 %). Deposit by the 7th of the next month (30 April for March); file the quarterly TDS statement and issue certificates. Rates and limits here are a learning summary — confirm them for real filings.

5TDS in TallyPrime, and TCS

  1. F11 → Taxation → Enable TDS; enter TAN and deductor details.
  2. Create TDS duty ledgers (Duties & Taxes, type TDS) per nature of payment; in expense and party ledgers set "TDS applicable" and the nature of payment.
  3. Enter the bill: TallyPrime computes TDS once the limit is crossed. Pay with the Stat Payment option; reports give the quarterly return data.

TCS (tax collected at source, now section 394) is collected by the seller on specified goods. For a manufacturer the common case is the sale of scrap; enable TCS and mark the scrap item or ledger when it applies, and check the current rate.
