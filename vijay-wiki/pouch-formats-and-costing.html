Pouch Formats and Costing: Film Area, Wastage, Conversion and Accessories

K Vijay Anand · Vijay_Wiki quick-reference article · 24 November 2026
PouchesCostingBag makingFlexible packaging

A pouch price is mostly material: the area of laminate it consumes multiplied by the laminate's cost per square metre, plus wastage. Conversion (bag making), accessories (zippers, spouts, valves) and margins add the rest. Quick, consistent area formulas per format make quotations fast and comparable.

Key takeaways

  • Film area per pouch depends on format: front + back panels plus gussets, seals and trims.
  • Laminate cost per m² = Σ layer grammage × price per kg ÷ 1,000 (see laminate builder article).
  • Wastage (start-up, trims, print waste) is typically 5–15 %; short runs carry more.
  • Stand-up and flat-bottom pouches cost more to make (slower machines) and often carry zippers or spouts.
  • Quote per thousand pouches and state dimensions as width × height (+ gusset) consistently.

2. Cost build-up

Material = area × laminate cost per m² × (1 + wastage) Pouch cost = material + conversion + accessories Price = cost × (1 + overhead) × (1 + margin)

3. Worked example

Stand-up pouch 160 × 230 mm with 40 mm bottom gusset (80 mm folded), laminate ₹25/m², 8 % wastage, conversion ₹0.60, zipper ₹0.45 (example rates).
