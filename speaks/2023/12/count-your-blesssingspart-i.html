Bliss is not same for everyone (Part-1)

Perception of bliss varies depending on your current state of mind and situation. Read the following cases and understand levels and forms of bliss.

  • For Anjali, her husband's survival from a major accident is a bliss.
  • For Bhavani her husband coming back home everyday is a bliss.
  • For Deepa, her husband not drinking everyday is a bliss.
  • For Kavitha, her husband giving money for running the family is a bliss.
  • For Meena, her husband spending quality time with her is a bliss.
  • For Nila her husband taking care of kids as well as her relatives is a bliss.
  • For Priya her husband giving her financial independence is a bliss.
  • For Sangeetha her husband listening to her words without hesitation is a bliss.
  • For Tharani her husband treating her as the queen of this world is a bliss.
  • For Vani, her husband knowing her limitations and acting accordingly is a bliss.
  • For Arjun his wife being with him though bedridden is a bliss.
  • For Karthik his wife preparing a meal for him is a bliss.
  • For Senthil his wife having trust in him is a bliss.
  • For Thiru his wife's understanding and listening nature is a bliss.
  • For Gokul his wife's beauty and politeness is a bliss.
  • For Pranav his wife listening to his words and decisions is a bliss.
  • For Vinai his wife allowing him to have private space at home is a bliss.
  • For Ashwin his wife being intimate with him twice a week is a bliss.
  • For Manoj his wife taking care of his parents also is a bliss.
  • For Suresh his wife knowing his limitations and act accordingly is a bliss.
