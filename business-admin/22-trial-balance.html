The Trial Balance and Errors

A trial balance is a list of every account balance. If total debits equal total credits, the arithmetic of double entry is right. But a balanced trial balance can still hide errors, and knowing which ones is a key accounting skill. Pick your level.

Level 3 · For experts — a review

Locating and correcting errors, and their effect on reported profit.

1Suspense account and rectification

If the trial balance does not agree and the error cannot be found before the deadline, the difference goes to a temporary suspense account. As each one-sided error is found, the correcting entry clears part of the suspense. Errors found after the accounts are closed are corrected through the profit-and-loss adjustment account (or as prior-period errors under Ind AS 8 / AS 5 if material).

3Divisible by 9

A transposition (writing 6,500 as 5,600) or slide (6,500 as 650) always produces a difference divisible by 9, since a number and any permutation of its digits are congruent mod 9 (digit-sum rule), and ×10ⁿ preserves the residue. If half the difference matches an amount in the books, look for an item posted on the wrong side.
