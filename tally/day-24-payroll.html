Payroll: Employees, Pay Heads, PF, ESI and Salary

Payroll in TallyPrime for Avani's forty people: employee groups and employees, pay heads for earnings and deductions, attendance, PF, ESI and professional tax, the salary voucher, payslips and the statutory reports.

1Today's goals

  • Create employee groups, employees and pay heads
  • Know how PF and ESI are calculated
  • Process salary with attendance in a payroll voucher
  • Book and pay the statutory dues
  • Print payslips and the pay sheet

2Payroll masters

MasterAvani's setup
Employee groups / categoriesProduction, Quality, Office
EmployeesName, joining date, designation, bank, PAN, UAN (PF), ESI number
Attendance / production typesPresent (days), Overtime (hours)
Pay headsEarnings: Basic, DA, HRA, Conveyance, Overtime · Deductions: Employee PF, Employee ESI, Professional Tax, TDS · Employer contributions: PF, ESI
Salary detailsEach employee's pay heads and amounts, effective from a date

3Statutory deductions

  • Provident Fund: 12 % of Basic + DA from the employee and 12 % from the employer (of which 8.33 % goes to the pension scheme, on wages up to ₹15,000), plus small admin/EDLI charges. Many employers cap PF wages at ₹15,000.
  • ESI: for employees earning up to ₹21,000 a month — 0.75 % employee, 3.25 % employer.
  • Professional tax: a state tax with state-specific slabs (Tamil Nadu collects it half-yearly).
  • Income tax (TDS on salary): now section 392 of the Income-tax Act, 2025.

4Lab: one machinist's salary

5Now in TallyPrime

  1. F11 → Enable payroll (and payroll statutory: PF, ESI, PT).
  2. Create employee groups, employees, units, attendance types and pay heads; then Salary Details for each employee.
  3. Enter attendance (Attendance voucher, from F10), then Payroll voucher (Ctrl+F4) → Auto Fill for the month and employee group → Accept.
  4. Pay the net salaries (Payment, Dr Salary Payable) and the PF / ESI dues by their due dates (15th of next month).
  5. Reports: Payslip, Pay Sheet, PF and ESI reports (Alt+G → Payroll Reports).
