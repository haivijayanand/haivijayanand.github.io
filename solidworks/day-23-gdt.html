Dimensions, Tolerances and GD&T

A drawing without good dimensions and tolerances is a picture. Import model dimensions, add tolerances and fits, datums and geometric tolerances.

⏱ about 2–3 hours🧪 Lab: Feature control frames❓ 5-question quiz

1Today's goals

  • Import dimensions with Model Items and arrange them
  • Add hole callouts, chamfer and ordinate dimensions
  • Apply tolerances and ISO fits (H7/g6…)
  • Build correct feature control frames and datum symbols

2Dimensions and tolerances

Annotation ▸ Model Items imports the dimensions you used in the model (driving dimensions — change them on the drawing and the model changes). Smart Dimension on a drawing adds reference (driven) dimensions in grey. Hole Callout reads Hole Wizard data. Also: chamfer dimensions, ordinate and baseline dimensions, centre marks and centrelines.

GD&T in one table

GroupCharacteristicsDatums?
FormStraightness, flatness, circularity, cylindricityNever
ProfileProfile of a line, profile of a surfaceOptional
OrientationAngularity, perpendicularity, parallelismRequired
LocationPosition (and in ISO: concentricity, symmetry)Required (position almost always)

Ⓜ (MMC) and Ⓛ (LMC) apply only to features of size (holes, pins, slots). ASME Y14.5-2018 removed concentricity and symmetry; ISO 1101 still uses them.

3Do it in SolidWorks

  1. Open the Day 22 pillow-block drawing. Model Items ▸ Source: Entire model ▸ Import items into all views. Delete duplicates; drag dimensions to the best view (Shift-drag moves between views).
  2. Hole Callout on the counterbores — the text comes from Hole Wizard.
  3. Bore Ø40: Tolerance/Precision ▸ Fit with tolerance, H7 → shows Ø40 H7 (+0.025/0).
  4. Annotation ▸ Datum Feature: A on the base bottom face, B on the bore axis.
  5. Annotation ▸ Geometric Tolerance: parallelism 0.02 to A on the bore axis; flatness 0.05 on the base (no datum).
  6. Position of the 2 mounting holes: Ø0.2 Ⓜ | A | B.
  7. Surface finish symbols: Ra 1.6 on the bore, Ra 3.2 general (in the title block note).

4Lab: feature control frame builder

Build frames and let the lab check them: a datum on a form tolerance, MMC on runout, or a Ø zone on flatness are all errors a checker would mark.

5Pro tips and common mistakes

  • Dimension for function and manufacture, not for whatever the model happened to use. Rework imported dimensions.
  • Never dimension to hidden lines if a section can show the feature.
  • Over-tolerancing costs money: start from general tolerances (ISO 2768-mK) in the title block and tighten only functional sizes.
  • DimXpert / MBD can put the same tolerances on the 3D model — useful when the CAM and CMM teams work from 3D.

6Practice

Fully dimension and tolerance the Day 5 shaft drawing: bearing seats Ø25 k6, circlip groove per DIN 471, keyway 8 P9, total runout 0.02 of bearing seats to datum A–B (the two seats), general tolerance ISO 2768-mK.
