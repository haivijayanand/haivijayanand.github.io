Differential Equations Part II: 100 Equations That Run the World

Part I taught how differential equations work. Part II is the atlas: the hundred equations that engineers, physicists, doctors, ecologists and economists actually use — from Newton's cooling and the Jeffcott rotor to the Reynolds equation of a journal bearing, Schrödinger's wave, the SIR epidemic and Black–Scholes. Each one gets its meaning, its symbols, a derivation for the major ones, a worked example with real numbers and a live lab you can play with.
