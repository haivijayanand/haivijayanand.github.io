28-Day SolidWorks Course · Week 4 · Day 28

Capstone Project, Final Assessment and Certificate

Put it all together: a single-stage spur gear unit from parts to assembly, drawings, BOM and a strength check. Then take the final assessment and print your certificate.

⏱ about 2–3 hours🧪 Lab: Exam + certificate🎓 Final assessment

1Today's goals

  • Plan a small product with a skeleton and design intent
  • Model, assemble, check and document it
  • Pass the 20-question final assessment
  • Know the next steps: CSWA and beyond

2Capstone brief: spur gear reducer

Design a single-stage spur gear reducer: input 1450 rpm, ratio about 2.5 : 1, module 2, pinion 17 teeth, gear 42 teeth (centre distance 59 mm), face width 20. Bearings 6205 on the input, 6206 on the output. Split housing with bolted cover.

3Deliverables checklist

  1. Skeleton part with named planes and a sketch of shaft centres (C = 59).
  2. Housing with 1–2° draft, uniform 8 mm walls, bores Ø52 H7 and Ø62 H7, 6 × M8 tapped holes.
  3. Cover built in context, references locked after completion.
  4. Shafts with bearing seats (k6), circlip grooves and keyways; pinion and gear.
  5. Assembly fully mated except the two shafts' rotation; gear mate 34 : 84.
  6. Interference report: only the intended bearing press fits.
  7. Exploded view in fitting order and a 10-second collapse animation.
  8. Drawings: housing, cover, both shafts, GA with indented BOM; all title blocks linked to properties.
  9. Mass properties of the whole unit; output shaft bending stress check with hand calculation + SimulationXpress.
  10. Pack and Go the whole project into one zip.

4Final assessment

Twenty questions from all four weeks. 70 % is the pass mark — the same mark used by the official SOLIDWORKS CAD Design Associate (CSWA) exam.

5Certificate

Type your name. The certificate shows how many days you have marked complete and your best final-assessment score. Print it or save as PDF.

6What next

  • SOLIDWORKS CAD Design Associate (CSWA): 3-hour hands-on exam, 70 % to pass. This course covers its sketching, part, assembly and drafting topics; practise speed with the sample exams.
  • CSWP (Professional) in three segments, then advanced certificates: Sheet Metal, Weldments, Surfacing, Drawing Tools, Mold Tools, and Simulation (CSWA-S).
  • Re-model a real part from your own work every week. Speed comes from repetition, design intent comes from being made to change your models.
  • Keep this course as a reference: every lab works offline once the page has loaded.
