How to play Spider

Two full decks (104 cards) are dealt into 10 columns: the first four get 6 cards and the other six get 5, with only the top card face up. The other 50 cards form the stock, dealt 10 at a time (one onto every column) — 5 deals in all.

  • Any face-up card may be placed on a card one rank higher of any suit (e.g. 7♥ on 8♠). A King or any run may go into an empty column.
  • Several cards move together only if they form a same-suit run in descending order (e.g. 9♠ 8♠ 7♠).
  • A complete same-suit run K down to A is removed automatically to the foundation. Clear all 8 runs to win.
  • When a face-down card is uncovered it turns face up.
  • You may not deal from the stock while any column is empty (standard rule) — fill gaps first.

Controls

Tap a card to send it (and the run on it) to the best place — a same-suit card is preferred, then any suit, then an empty column. Or drag it with mouse or finger. Undo is unlimited. Hint highlights a useful move (tap again for the next one). Tap the blue stock pile to deal.

Scoring

Start with 500. Every move and every deal costs −1. Each completed K→A run earns +100. Undo restores the position and score exactly as it was (variant choice: no undo penalty).

From your point of view every hidden card (stock + face-down) is equally likely to be in the 10 dealt cards, so the count of “outs” in a deal is hypergeometric.

Strategy tips

  • Prefer moves that turn over a face-down card — information is everything in Spider.
  • Build same-suit whenever you have a choice; mixed-suit stacks cannot move as a unit.
  • Empty columns are your most valuable resource: use them to re-order runs, don't fill them with a lone King too early.
  • Work on the columns with the fewest face-down cards first to create an empty column.
  • Before dealing, make every useful move — a deal buries the top of every column.
  • Kings can only be moved into empty columns, so a King on top of face-down cards is a problem; uncover early.
