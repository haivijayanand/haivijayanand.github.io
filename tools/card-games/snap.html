How to play Snap

  1. The 52-card deck is dealt face-down as evenly as possible to everyone (you plus 1–3 computer players: Arun, Meena, Ravi).
  2. In turn, each player flips the top card of their stack onto the middle pile. On your turn tap Flip my card (or press F / Enter).
  3. When the card just flipped has the same rank as the card under it (e.g. 7♠ on 7♥), hit SNAP! (or Space). The first player to snap wins the whole pile, added to the bottom of their stack.
  4. Wrong snap (no match): you give one card to each opponent as a penalty.
  5. A player with no cards left skips their turn to flip, but can still SNAP to win cards and get back in.
  6. If every stack is empty and there's no snap, the player who flipped last turns the pile over and keeps going — when they hold all 52, they win.

Modes

  • Win all cards — play until one player holds all 52 cards.
  • 3-minute race — when the clock runs out, the player holding the most cards wins (cards in the middle pile don't count).

Odds & Strategy

Why 3/51 on a fresh deck?

After the first card is flipped, 51 cards remain unseen. Exactly 3 of them share its rank (there are 4 of each rank). So the next flip matches with probability 3/51 = 1/17 ≈ 5.88%. Over a whole shuffled deck there are 51 neighbouring pairs, so the expected number of snap chances in one pass is 51 × 3/51 = 3.

1 · Chance the next flip matches

If U cards are still unseen and m of them share the top card's rank:
P(match) = m / U

2 · Flips between snaps (geometric)

Treat each flip as an independent trial with match chance p. The number of flips until the next match X is geometric:
P(X = k) = (1−p)^(k−1) · p, E[X] = 1/p, P(X ≤ k) = 1 − (1−p)^k

Strategy tips

  • Watch the rank, not the suit — 7♠ on 7♥ is a snap.
  • Snaps come about once every 17 flips on average — stay alert, but don't guess: a wrong snap costs a card to every opponent.
  • Against Hard players, keep your finger on the button (or Space) — they react in ~0.3–0.55 s.
  • In the 3-minute race, a wrong snap with 3 opponents costs you 3 cards — only snap when sure.
