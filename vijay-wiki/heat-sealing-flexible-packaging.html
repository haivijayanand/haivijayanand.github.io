Heat Sealing of Flexible Packaging: Temperature, Pressure, Dwell and Seal Windows

K Vijay Anand · Vijay_Wiki quick-reference article · 24 November 2026
Heat sealingPackagingHot tackPouches

A heat seal forms when the sealant layers of two webs melt at their interface, interdiffuse under pressure, and cool while held together. The jaw temperature is easy to set, but what matters is the temperature reached at the interface, which depends on dwell time and on how much material the heat must cross. That is why thick laminates need longer dwell or hotter jaws than thin films.

Key takeaways

  • Seal quality depends on three variables: jaw temperature, dwell time and pressure. Temperature and time dominate.
  • Seal initiation temperature (SIT) depends on the sealant: about 90–100 °C for mLLDPE and ionomers, 105–115 °C for LDPE, 125–140 °C for CPP.
  • Heat must conduct through outer layers; interface heating time scales with thickness² / thermal diffusivity.
  • Hot tack (strength while still hot) matters on vertical form-fill-seal lines where product drops on a fresh seal.
  • Measure seal strength by ASTM F88 / EN 868-5 (N per 15 mm or 25 mm) and plot strength against temperature to find the window.

1. The seal window

SealantApprox. seal initiation (°C)Notes
Ionomer85–95Seals through contamination, excellent hot tack
EVA (high VA)80–95Low temperature, soft
mLLDPE90–100Strong hot tack, low SIT
LDPE105–115General purpose
LLDPE110–120Tougher than LDPE
Heat-seal coated BOPP105–125Narrow window; acrylic or terpolymer coatings
CPP125–140Retort and higher temperature
PET (APET, sealable)140–160Special grades

The upper limit of the window is set by distortion, burn-through, shrinkage of the outer film or degradation; outer films such as PET and BOPP must tolerate jaw temperature without sticking.

4. Defects and causes

DefectLikely cause
Weak / peelable sealInterface too cold: dwell short, jaw cold, thick laminate
Burnt, shrunk, wrinkled sealTemperature too high, outer film not heat-resistant
Channel leaksWrinkles, gussets, uneven pressure, worn jaws
Seal through product failsContamination: use ionomer/mLLDPE or ultrasonic
Delamination near sealLaminate bond weak or adhesive not cured
