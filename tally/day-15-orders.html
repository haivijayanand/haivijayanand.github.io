Order Processing: Purchase and Sales Orders, Receipt and Delivery Notes

Order processing for a manufacturer: Purchase Orders to suppliers, Receipt Notes (goods received), Rejections Out, then the purchase invoice; Sales Orders from customers, Delivery Notes, then the sales invoice — and the reports that show what is still pending.

⏱ about 2 hours🧪 Lab: Order-to-invoice flow❓ 10-question quiz

1Today's goals

  • Switch on purchase and sales order processing
  • Raise a Purchase Order and receive goods against it in part
  • Return rejected goods with Rejections Out
  • Take a Sales Order and deliver against it
  • Read pending-order and pending-bill reports

2Why orders

Orders and notes do not touch the accounts — they record promises and physical movements. That is exactly what a factory needs: stores knows what is due in, planning knows what customers are waiting for, and accounts can check every bill against what was ordered and actually received (the "three-way match").

BuyingKeyEffectSellingKeyEffect
Purchase OrderCtrl+F9Promise to buySales OrderCtrl+F8Promise to supply
Receipt Note (GRN)Alt+F9Stock inDelivery NoteAlt+F8Stock out

The link is the order number and the tracking number (the receipt or delivery note number): each later voucher picks the earlier one from a list, so quantities carry forward and pending balances are always known.

3Lab: order-to-invoice flow

A purchase order for bearings and oil seals, part delivery, a rejection and two invoices. Press S for each event and watch the pending columns.

4Practice Desk: order vouchers

5Now in TallyPrime

  1. F11 → Inventory → Enable purchase order processing and sales order processing; also "Use tracking numbers" (delivery / receipt notes).
  2. Enter the five vouchers above. In the Receipt Note choose the order number PO-07; in the Delivery Note pick the sales order.
  3. Open Alt+G → Purchase Orders Outstanding (80 bearings pending) and Sales Bills Pending (the delivery not yet invoiced).
  4. Raise the purchase invoice selecting the tracking number GRN-31 — quantities fill in by themselves.
