Industrial Communication: Modbus RTU/TCP, RS-485, CAN and OPC UA

K Vijay Anand · Vijay_Wiki quick-reference article · 24 November 2026
ModbusRS-485CANOPC UA

Test rigs connect dynamometer controllers, drives, power meters and PLCs over a handful of standard links. Most problems are simple once you know the conventions: which register numbering the manual uses, which word order a 32-bit float has, and whether the RS-485 line is terminated and biased. This article collects those conventions in one place.

Key takeaways

  • Modbus holding register 40001 is address 0 in the protocol (function 03); manuals mix 0- and 1-based numbering.
  • A 32-bit float spans two 16-bit registers; word order varies (ABCD, CDAB, BADC, DCBA): try swaps if values look absurd.
  • RS-485: daisy-chain topology, 120 Ω termination at both ends, failsafe biasing, common ground reference, up to 32 unit loads and about 1,200 m at low baud rates.
  • Modbus RTU frames are separated by 3.5 character times of silence; at 19,200 baud reading 20 registers takes about 30 ms.
  • CAN trades speed for distance (1 Mbit/s to about 40 m, 125 kbit/s to about 500 m); OPC UA provides secure, modelled data over Ethernet.

Float32 = registers R(n), R(n+1) → bytes A B C D Orders: ABCD (big-endian), CDAB (word swap), BADC (byte swap), DCBA (little-endian) RTU character = 11 bits (start + 8 data + parity/stop + stop); frame gap ≥ 3.5 characters

Example timing at 19,200 baud reading 20 registers: request 4.6 ms, response 25.8 ms, gaps 4.0 ms → about 34 ms per poll before device response delay.

2. RS-485 wiring

  • Twisted pair with shield; connect signal ground (common) between devices.
  • Termination 120 Ω at both ends; failsafe bias resistors at one point so the idle line is defined.
  • Unique addresses 1–247; all devices at the same baud rate, parity and stop bits.
  • USB-to-RS-485 adapters: check automatic direction control and isolation.

OPC UA runs over Ethernet with encryption, user authentication and an information model (variables with units, ranges and structure). It suits connecting PLCs, data acquisition and higher-level systems without vendor-specific drivers.
