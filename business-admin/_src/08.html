3Giving back

Many businesses help their community: a company might build toilets in schools, plant trees, or run free eye camps. In India, big companies must spend part of their profit on such work. This is called Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR).

4Words to remember

1India's CSR rule: Section 135

A company must spend on CSR if, in the previous financial year, it had net worth of ₹500 crore or more, or turnover of ₹1,000 crore or more, or net profit of ₹5 crore or more. It must spend at least 2% of its average net profit of the last three years on activities in Schedule VII (hunger, health, education, environment, rural development, sports, heritage and others).

CSR obligation calculator
  • If the obligation is ₹50 lakh or less, the board can handle it without a separate CSR committee.
  • Unspent money for an ongoing project goes to an Unspent CSR Account within 30 days of year-end, to be used within three years; otherwise it goes to a Schedule VII fund (such as the PM CARES or PM's National Relief Fund) within six months.
  • Activities in the normal course of business, or only for employees, do not count.

2Your rights as a consumer

The Consumer Protection Act, 2019 gives six rights: to safety, to be informed, to choose, to be heard, to seek redress, and to consumer education. Complaints go to District, State or National Consumer Commissions (by value of goods or services: up to ₹50 lakh, ₹50 lakh–₹2 crore, above ₹2 crore), and can be filed online through e-Daakhil. The Central Consumer Protection Authority acts against misleading ads and unfair trade practices, including "dark patterns" in apps.

3BRSR and the ESG debate

SEBI requires the top 1,000 listed companies by market cap to file a Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report structured on the nine principles of the National Guidelines on Responsible Business Conduct. BRSR Core, a subset of key indicators (emissions, water, waste, gender diversity, wellbeing spending), requires reasonable assurance, phased in from the largest companies; value-chain disclosures follow later on a comply-or-explain basis.

Does ESG create value? Meta-analyses (Friede, Busch & Bassen 2015) find mostly non-negative links between ESG and financial performance, but ratings disagree heavily across providers (Berg, Kölbel & Rigobon 2022 find correlations around 0.5), and "materiality" matters: only issues material to the industry predict returns (Khan, Serafeim & Yoon 2016). Critics argue ESG blurs accountability; supporters argue it prices externalities markets ignore.
