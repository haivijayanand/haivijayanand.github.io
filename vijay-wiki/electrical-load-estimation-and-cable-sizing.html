Electrical Load Estimation: Demand, kVA, Transformer and Cable Sizing

K Vijay Anand · Vijay_Wiki quick-reference article · 24 November 2026
ElectricalLoad estimationCablesTransformers

Every facility plan needs an electrical load estimate early, to size the transformer, the incoming supply, cables and backup generation. The connected load (everything that could run) is always larger than the maximum demand (what actually runs together), and the gap is captured by demand and diversity factors.

Key takeaways

  • Maximum demand = Σ (connected kW × demand factor); further diversity between groups reduces it again.
  • kVA = kW / power factor; line current I = kVA × 1000/(√3 × V). At 415 V, 1 kVA ≈ 1.39 A.
  • Size transformers about 20–30 % above maximum demand, and check large motor starting.
  • Direct-on-line motor starting draws 6–7 × full-load current; soft starters and drives reduce it.
  • Cable size must satisfy both current rating (with derating) and voltage drop (typically ≤ 3–5 %).
