Graha Bala Spider Plots

Continuous 360° strength curves for the seven classical planets — Ucca Bala (exaltation strength, by zodiac degree) and Dik Bala (directional strength, by house). Drawn in South Indian orientation: Aries fills the 11–12 hour on the clock face, Taurus starts at 12, and the signs run clockwise in whole-hour steps.

Newtonian attraction between each body and the Earth, computed as F ∝ M₁M₂ / r², expressed as a percentage of the pull the Moon exerts on the Earth at its mean distance. Mass is in Earth masses; distance is in Earth–Moon distances, where 1 unit = 384,400 km.

The twelve bhāvas counted from the lagna. Each sector is a whole hour on the clock face — house 1 sits at 11–12 by default, the next at 12–1, running clockwise. The rāśis on the outer ring are fixed to their sectors in South Indian fashion, so clicking a sector moves the lagna there and the houses renumber clockwise from it.

This wheel always uses the South Indian clock layout, so the orientation toggle above does not apply here. House 1 belongs to both groups at once — it is the only bhāva that is both a kendra and a trikoṇa, which is why the tradition treats the lagna as the single most important point in the chart. The upachaya houses (3, 6, 10, 11) form a cross-cutting group said to improve with time, so a malefic placed there is read differently from the same malefic elsewhere. The kāraka column gives the planet most commonly assigned as natural significator; sources differ on several houses, and some assign more than one, so treat that column as indicative rather than settled.

Opposite pairs invert. Sun (ucca Aries) and Saturn (ucca Libra) are near-mirror images, as are Jupiter (Cancer) and Mars (Capricorn). Where one planet peaks the other bottoms out. Mercury is the outlier the classics flag: it is exalted in Virgo and debilitated in Pisces, and its 15° peak sits close to a quarter-turn from most others — the curve here uses the standard 180° axis model throughout, so Mercury's profile is the same shape, just rotated.

Every curve is the same cardioid rotated to its own exaltation point. The seven do not sum to a constant: the combined total peaks at 413.9% around Taurus 3° and bottoms at 286.1% around Scorpio 3°.

Directional strength

House 1 fills the 11–12 hour and the houses run clockwise in whole-hour steps, matching the sign wheel. Strength is measured outward from each planet's powerless cusp.

Which planet is strong where

Dik Bala answers a different question from exaltation. Exaltation asks how comfortably a planet expresses its nature; direction asks in which sphere of life it has leverage. A Sun in Aries inside the 10th house has both — peak sign alignment and full directional power.

Houses influenced

Counting is inclusive: a planet in the 1st casting a 7th-house aspect lands on house 7. Strength follows the Parāśarī scale; the three special aspects override it to full.
