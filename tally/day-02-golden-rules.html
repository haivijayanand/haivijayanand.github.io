Double Entry and the Golden Rules

Double entry: every transaction has a debit and an equal credit. Learn the three golden rules (real, personal, nominal accounts) and the modern rule (assets and expenses increase by debit) — the only theory you need to make any entry in TallyPrime.

⏱ about 2 hours🧪 Lab: Golden rules trainer❓ 10-question quiz

1Today's goals

  • Explain debit (Dr) and credit (Cr) without the words "good" or "bad"
  • Classify an account as real, personal or nominal
  • Apply the three golden rules
  • Use the modern rule as a cross-check
  • Decide the Dr and Cr of twelve Avani transactions

2Debit and credit are just left and right

Every account has two sides. The left side is called debit (Dr), the right side credit (Cr). They are positions, not judgements. A debit to the bank account is good news (money in); a debit to the wages account is money spent. Double entry says: in every transaction, total debits = total credits.

Debit comes from Latin debere, "to owe"; credit from credere, "to trust". The person who owes us is a debtor; the person who trusted us with goods is a creditor.

3The three golden rules

Account typeWhat it isDebitCredit
RealThings — cash, bank, machinery, stock, buildingWhat comes inWhat goes out
PersonalPeople and bodies — customers, suppliers, banks as lenders, capital (the owners)The receiverThe giver
NominalIncomes, expenses, gains, losses — wages, rent, sales, interestAll expenses and lossesAll incomes and gains
Note on Bank: In TallyPrime a bank account is under Bank Accounts and behaves like cash (real): money in is a debit. Purchases and Sales are treated as nominal (expense / income) accounts.

4The modern rule — a cross-check

Increase by debitIncrease by credit
Assets · Expenses (and losses)Liabilities · Capital · Income (and gains)

Use either rule — they always agree. The modern rule is often faster: "machine is an asset and it increases → debit".
