SMART goal checker

OKRs (objectives and key results) pair one inspiring objective with 2–4 measurable key results. Score each key result 0–1 at the end of the quarter; landing at 0.6–0.7 means the target was ambitious enough.

1Levels and schools of strategy

LevelQuestionTools
CorporateWhich businesses should we be in? How do we add value as a parent?Portfolio (BCG, GE-McKinsey), parenting advantage, M&A
BusinessHow do we compete in this market?Generic strategies (cost, differentiation, focus), value stick, five forces

Mintzberg's distinction: intended strategy becomes deliberate (realised as planned) or unrealised, while emergent strategy arises from patterns in action. Most realised strategies mix both. The design school (Andrews: SWOT fit), planning school (Ansoff), positioning school (Porter), and learning school (Mintzberg, Quinn's logical incrementalism) emphasise different parts of the same process.
