Taking a course? Sign in to keep your progress

My Progress → My Certificates →
It's free with your Google account. Once you're signed in:
  • Pick up where you stopped: every course day, chapter and review remembers your place.
  • See what's done: ✓ ticks and "completed" counts on each course's contents page.
  • Keep your certificates: saved to your account, reprint them or share a verify link any time.
  • Use any device: start on the PC, continue on the phone.
Only your name, email and reading progress are stored. Sign out any time from the account button at the top.
