How the Two lines tab works

1 · Place the vertex. Your first tap marks the point O where the lines cross.

2 · Draw rays. Each tap after that shoots a ray out of O, named A, B, C, D… in order. Drag a ray tip to swing it; drag O to move the whole figure.

3 · Measure. Tap Measure angle, then tap a ray — it becomes a vector with its tail at O and an arrowhead at the far end. Tap a second ray to get the angle. Double tap empty space to let go of the selection.

4 · Keep several at once. Keep this angle pins the measurement to the board so you can build up all four angles at a crossing and check they add to 360°. Tick Keep every angle to pin them automatically; tap a pin below the board to remove it.

Why order matters: ∠AOB is the turn from OA to OB. Start at OB instead and you sweep the rest of the circle, so the two add up to 360°.

Keyboard: 1–6 pick a ray, Esc clears the selection.

How the Polygon tab works

Sides, diagonals and O. Every side has its own colour and the corners are named A, B, C… anticlockwise. Grey dashed lines are the diagonals. In a quadrilateral the two diagonals cross at a single point and that point is O; in bigger polygons each crossing is a named junction (X1, X2…) and O sits at their middle. All of it is recomputed the instant you move a corner.

Measure at a corner. Tap two lines that share a corner — both tails jump to it and the angle reads ∠ABC: first letter, corner, second letter.

Measure where diagonals cross. Tap two diagonals that cross without touching, and the angle is taken at the crossing itself, so a square gives ∠AOB = 90°. Each vector points toward the end you tapped, so tapping the other half gives the vertically opposite angle instead.

See them all. All corner angles labels every corner and checks the total against (n−2)×180°. Split corners by diagonals cuts each corner into the pieces the diagonals make, so you can see the side-to-diagonal angles adding back up to the corner. Junction angles opens up every diagonal crossing — the four angles there add to 360° and opposite ones are equal.

Drag the corners. The shape name updates live. Tick marks show equal sides, a small square shows a right angle, and double tap empty space drops the selection.
