30-Day TallyPrime Course · Week 5 · Day 30

Capstone: A Month at Avani Gearworks, Final Assessment and Certificate

Put it all together: one month-end at Avani Gearworks — purchases with GST, production, sales, collections and payments, job work with TDS, a scrap sale and bank charges — in the Practice Desk. Then the 20-question final assessment and your certificate.

⏱ about 2 hours🧪 Lab: Exam + certificate🎓 Final assessment

1Today's goals

  • Choose the right voucher for each event without hints
  • Enter a month of mixed transactions accurately
  • Check the trial balance of your entries
  • Pass the final assessment
  • Know where to go next

2Capstone: March 2027 at Avani Gearworks

Eleven events. Each one needs a different decision: which voucher, which ledgers, which items, which tax. Use the hint only if you are stuck; check the trial balance at the end.

3Where to go next

  • Repeat the whole course's entries in your own TallyPrime (Educational mode) and compare your Balance Sheet with the Practice Desk trial balances.
  • Read the TallyPrime help on GST returns, TDS and payroll for your release; tax rules change every year.
  • Tally Education's certification programmes and the TallyPrime help site are the official next steps; for accounting depth, a book on financial accounting and the ICAI study material are excellent.
  • Pair this course with the site's Manufacturing and Stock Management courses — Avani's numbers make more sense when you know the factory behind them.

4Final assessment

Twenty questions across the whole course. Your best score is saved and printed on the certificate.

5Your certificate

Type your name as you want it printed. The certificate counts the days you marked complete and your best final-assessment score. Signed in? Save it to your account for a verifiable certificate ID.
