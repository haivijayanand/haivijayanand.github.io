    A plain journal bearing's oil film turns at about half the shaft speed. Push the shaft sideways and the film pushes back at right angles — forward along the whirl. This cross-coupled force feeds energy into a forward whirl at the natural frequency. Damping drains energy at a rate ∝ ωn; the film feeds it at a rate ∝ Ω/2. When Ω exceeds about 2ωn feeding wins: the orbit grows (oil whip) and locks at the critical frequency however fast the shaft turns.
