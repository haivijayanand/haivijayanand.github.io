Purchase and Sales with Inventory (Item Invoice)

Purchase and Sales in Item Invoice mode: choose the party and ledger, then the items with quantity and rate — one entry updates the accounts, the stock, the registers and (from Week 4) GST. Plus sales returns with items.

⏱ about 2 hours🧪 Lab: Practice Desk: item invoices❓ 10-question quiz

1Today's goals

  • Record a purchase invoice with items
  • Record a sales invoice with several items
  • See stock quantities change in the Stock Summary
  • Record a sales return with items in a Credit Note
  • Know where the sales or purchase ledger comes from

2Item invoice

In Item Invoice mode a Sales voucher (F8) asks for: Party A/c name (the customer, or Cash) → Sales ledger → item lines (Name of item, Quantity, Rate, Amount, and godown if multiple) → ledgers below the items (GST, freight, round off) → narration. TallyPrime then:

  • debits the party and credits the sales ledger (accounts),
  • reduces the stock of each item in its godown (inventory),
  • adds a line to the Sales Register and (with GST) to GSTR-1.

A Purchase (F9) is the mirror image. GST ledgers are added in Week 4; this week's invoices are before tax so you can see the stock effect clearly.

3Practice Desk: item invoices

In the desk, type the party, the ledger and each item's quantity and rate (item names come from the list). Leave the tax lines empty this week.

4Now in TallyPrime

  1. Vouchers → F9; if it opens as an accounting invoice, Ctrl+H → Item Invoice.
  2. Enter the three purchases, the two sales and the credit note. Choose the godown for each item when asked.
  3. Open Stock Summary and check: EN19 up 3,000 kg, gearboxes down 44 net. Then Alt+G → Stock Item → Gearbox GB-20 for its movement.
  4. Press Alt+P on a sales invoice to see the printed invoice.
