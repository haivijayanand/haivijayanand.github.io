    Method notes

    Level 1 — blend inside a layer

    Pblend = Mm(wi, Pi) + Σ wAwB·(PA+PB)/2·(a0 + a1x + a2x²), where Mm is the weighted power mean of order m: m = 1 linear (Voigt), m = 0 logarithmic, m = −1 inverse (Reuss). Defaults: modulus, yield, break strength and tear use m = 1; elongation m = 0; dart m = −1 (weakest-phase dominated). Excess terms act between resin families (ZN-LLDPE, mLLDPE, LDPE, HDPE). Their values are illustrative: they reproduce the trend shapes reported in the literature and should be refitted to your own data.

    Density is exact: specific volumes add. Fillers: modulus × (1 + 2.5φ + 14.1φ²) (Guth); yield, break strength and elongation × (1 − 1.21φ2/3) (Nicolais–Narkis, no adhesion, a lower bound). Dart and tear are not corrected for filler.

    Level 2 — layers in the coextruded film

    Layers are strained together in the film plane, so modulus and strengths add by thickness fraction (iso-strain). Fracture properties do not add: dart and tear are reported between the inverse (series) and linear (parallel) bounds, with the thickness-weighted log mean as the central value.

    Cost

    Material cost of a layer is the wt %-weighted price of its ingredients (masterbatch priced as supplied). Film material cost weights layers by mass (thickness × density). Net waste cost = material × w/(1−w) − scrap credit × w/(1−w). Conversion = specific energy × tariff + labour per hour ÷ output + (straight-line depreciation + interest on half the line cost) ÷ running hours ÷ output + overheads + packing. Rs/m² = Rs/kg × grammage. All prices are indicative and ex-GST.

    Why the method fails

    Toughness depends on tie-chain density, orientation and crack initiation, not on composition alone. Datasheet values come from different lines and BURs; LDPE changes the orientation frozen in by the bubble; HDPE forms its own crystals; fillers act through dispersion and adhesion; and in a coextrusion the first layer to crack controls the film. Use the predictions to rank and screen formulations, then confirm on the line.
