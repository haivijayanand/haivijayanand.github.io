    From trial balance to final accounts

    Every ledger's group decides where it lands (Day 6). Sales, purchases and direct items make the trading section and gross profit; indirect incomes and expenses take it to net profit; everything else — capital, loans, assets, debtors, creditors, taxes payable — forms the Balance Sheet, which must tally.

    Gross profit = Sales + Direct incomes + Closing stock − (Opening stock + Purchases + Direct expenses)
    Net profit = Gross profit + Indirect incomes − Indirect expenses

    In TallyPrime, with inventory integrated, closing stock comes from the Stock Summary automatically. Without inventory you would enter it in the Stock-in-Hand ledger each period.

    Interest on overdue bills

    Avani's terms say 18 % a year on bills unpaid after 30 days. With F11 → Enable interest calculation and, in each customer ledger, Activate interest calculation = Yes (rate 18 % per 365-day year, applicable from 30 days after the bill date), TallyPrime's Interest Calculation report shows the interest due by bill. Book it with a Debit Note or Journal (Dr customer, Cr Interest Received).

    Credit limits

    Set a credit limit for each customer (for example ₹5 lakh for Malabar Agro): Alt+G → Multi Alter → Credit Limits or in the ledger. When a sales invoice would take the balance over the limit, TallyPrime warns before you accept — a cheap way to avoid the next bad debt.

    Foreign currency

    Avani starts exporting SG-40 gear sets to a buyer in Sri Lanka, invoiced in US dollars.

    1. F11 → Enable multiple currencies. Create the currency $ (US Dollar, 2 decimals).
    2. Set rates in Rates of Exchange for the date (standard, selling, buying rates).
    3. In the customer ledger choose currency $. The sales invoice shows dollar amounts and their rupee value.
    4. On receipt, TallyPrime uses the day's rate; the difference is the forex gain or loss (see Forex Gain/Loss report), booked with a journal.

    Security

    • Users and roles (Alt+K → User Roles / Users and Passwords): the stores clerk can enter receipt notes but not see the Profit & Loss; the accountant can enter vouchers but not alter last month; the owner sees everything.
    • TallyVault (Alt+K → TallyVault): encrypts the company data with a password. If the TallyVault password is lost, the data cannot be recovered — store it safely.
    • Edit Log: companies in India must keep an audit trail of every change to their books (Companies (Accounts) Rules, from 1 April 2023). TallyPrime's Edit Log records each creation, alteration and deletion with user and time; it cannot be switched off once required.

    Data

    • Backup (Alt+Y → Backup): to another drive, daily; keep one copy off the premises or in the cloud (TallyPrime 7 shows the TallyDrive backup status).
    • Restore (Alt+Y → Restore) into a different folder first, check, then use.
    • Repair (Alt+Y → Repair) after a power failure or data error.
    • Migrate old Tally.ERP 9 data to TallyPrime with the migration tool.
    • Export (Alt+E): Excel, PDF, XML, JSON. Import (Alt+O): masters and transactions from Excel or XML — useful for loading 200 stock items at once.
    • Print (Alt+P) and share (Alt+M) by e-mail; send invoices and reminders on WhatsApp from TallyPrime.

    Depreciation

    Machines wear out; depreciation spreads their cost over their useful life. Two methods:

    • Straight line (SLM): the same amount every year — (cost − residual value) ÷ life. The Companies Act's Schedule II uses useful lives (for example 15 years for general plant).
    • Written-down value (WDV): a fixed percentage of the remaining book value — large at first, smaller later. Income-tax uses WDV rates on blocks of assets (15 % for general plant and machinery), with half the rate in the year of purchase if the asset is used for less than 180 days.

    Year-end checklist

    1. Physical stock count; record differences (Physical Stock).
    2. Bank reconciliation for every account up to 31 March.
    3. Confirm balances with major customers and suppliers; clear On Account items.
    4. Depreciation; provisions for expenses (audit, wages, power); prepaid and accrued items; interest accrued on loans.
    5. GST: reconcile books with GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and GSTR-2B; TDS: all deducted and deposited.
    6. Check exception reports (negative stock and ledgers); review the Edit Log.
    7. Print or export the final accounts; take a backup labelled "FY 2026-27 closing".

    Practice Desk: year-end entries

    The new year

    TallyPrime does not need closing entries. On 1 April 2027 simply enter vouchers with the new date: assets, liabilities and capital carry forward, income and expense ledgers start again at zero, and the year's profit moves into the Profit & Loss A/c balance in the Balance Sheet (a company may transfer it to Reserves & Surplus with a journal). Change the reporting period with Alt+F2. Use Alt+Y → Split only when a company's data grows too large or the auditor wants a sealed year.
