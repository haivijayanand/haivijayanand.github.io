28-Day Creo Parametric Course · Week 1 · Day 06

Rounds, Chamfers and Shell

Rounds, chamfers and shells turn blocks into real parts. Learn Creo's round sets, Auto Round, the chamfer schemes, and Shell with removed surfaces and non-default thicknesses.

⏱ about 2–3 hours🧪 Lab: Round vs chamfer, 3D shell❓ 5-question quiz

1Today's goals

  • Make rounds with several sets and radii, and variable radii
  • Know the order: draft, then big rounds, then shell, then small rounds
  • Use Edge Chamfer schemes: D × D, D1 × D2, Angle × D, 45 × D
  • Shell a part, remove faces and give some faces a different thickness
  • Use Auto Round for cast parts

2Rounds, chamfers and shells

RoundModel ▸ Round. Pick edges; each group with one radius is a set. Ctrl-click adds edges to the set. The Sets panel adds new sets, variable radii and conic/curvature-continuous shapes.
ChamferModel ▸ Chamfer ▸ Edge Chamfer. Schemes D × D, D1 × D2, Angle × D, 45 × D. Corner Chamfer is for vertices.
ShellModel ▸ Shell. Hollows the part to a thickness; picked surfaces are removed (open). Non-default thickness gives chosen faces another value.
Auto RoundRounds every edge (or all concave/convex ones) with one radius. Great for castings — put it near the end of the tree.
Order matters. A good order for a moulded or cast part: base shape → draft → large rounds → shell → ribs and bosses → small rounds. Shell copies the rounds to the inside, so round first if you want rounded inside corners.

Area removed by a round of radius R at a 90° corner = (1 − π/4)R² ≈ 0.215 R²; by a D1 × D2 chamfer = ½·D1·D2.

3Do it in Creo

  1. New part tray. Extrude a 150 × 100 × 40 block, centred on RIGHT and FRONT.
  2. Round: pick the four vertical edges (Ctrl-click) → R15. One set.
  3. In the same Round, open Sets ▸ New set, pick the bottom edge loop (pick one edge, Shift-click for the chain) → R5. Finish: one Round feature, two sets.
  4. Shell: pick the top face to remove, thickness 3. Open the References panel ▸ Non-default thickness → pick the bottom face → 5. Finish.
  5. Edge Chamfer the top outside edge loop: 45 × D, D = 1.
  6. Try a variable-radius round: on the tray rim, in the Sets panel right-click the radius list ▸ Add radius and set 2 at one end, 6 at the other.
  7. Edit Definition of the first Round and change R15 to R30. Shell follows — the wall stays 3.

4Lab: round vs chamfer, and shell in 3D

Now shell a block and change the wall thickness and removed faces.

5Pro tips and common mistakes

  • Round edges last where possible: rounds are often what breaks when earlier geometry changes (Day 15).
  • Shell fails if a radius on the outside is smaller than the shell thickness — the inside round would have negative radius. Increase the radius or reduce thickness.
  • Use intent edges / surface chains (Shift-pick) so new edges get rounded too when the shape changes.
  • Chamfers on drawings: Creo can show a 45 × D chamfer dimension as "1 × 45°" — set it up in detail options.
  • Do not round every edge in a machined part — every round is a tool change. Rounds on castings, chamfers on machined edges.

6Practice

  1. Model a cast cover: 200 × 120 × 50, R20 vertical corners, R5 bottom edges, shell 4 with the bottom open, flange 10 wide around the open edge (extrude a thin profile).
  2. Add an Auto Round R2 at the end of the tree for all remaining concave edges.
  3. Change the shell from 4 to 6 and the corners from R20 to R10. Fix anything that fails.
