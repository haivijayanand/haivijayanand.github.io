Groups and Ledgers

The chart of accounts: TallyPrime's 28 ready-made groups, how to create your own ledgers under them, opening balances, and why the right group decides where an amount lands in the Balance Sheet or Profit & Loss.

⏱ about 2 hours🧪 Lab: Ledger-to-group sorter❓ 10-question quiz

1Today's goals

  • Know the 15 primary groups and the 13 sub-groups
  • Know which groups feed the Balance Sheet and which the Profit & Loss
  • Create a ledger with the right group and opening balance
  • Create a group of your own under a ready-made one
  • Place twenty Avani ledgers in their groups

2The 28 ready-made groups

TallyPrime gives you 28 groups: 15 primary and 13 sub-groups. Most companies never need more than a few of their own. Two ledgers exist from the start: Cash and Profit & Loss A/c.

Balance Sheet — primary (9)Capital Account · Loans (Liability) · Current Liabilities · Fixed Assets · Investments · Current Assets · Branch / Divisions · Misc. Expenses (Asset) · Suspense A/c
Profit & Loss — primary (6)Sales Accounts · Purchase Accounts · Direct Incomes · Direct Expenses · Indirect Incomes · Indirect Expenses
Sub-groups (13)Reserves & Surplus (Capital) · Bank OD A/c, Secured Loans, Unsecured Loans (Loans) · Duties & Taxes, Provisions, Sundry Creditors (Current Liabilities) · Bank Accounts, Cash-in-Hand, Deposits (Asset), Loans & Advances (Asset), Stock-in-Hand, Sundry Debtors (Current Assets)
Direct vs indirect. Direct expenses relate to making the product (factory wages, power, freight inward, job work) and decide gross profit. Indirect expenses run the business (office salaries, rent of the office, audit fees) and come after gross profit.

3Create a ledger

  1. Alt+G → Create Master → Ledger (or Gateway → Create → Ledger).
  2. Name: Sri Murugan Steels. Under: Sundry Creditors.
  3. For parties: Maintain balances bill-by-bill = Yes, default credit period (for example 30 days), mailing details, state and GSTIN (from Week 4).
  4. Opening balance as on 1-4-2026, Dr or Cr (for example ₹1,20,000 Cr — Avani owed this on day one).
  5. Ctrl+A. Create several at once with Alt+G → Create Master → Multi Ledgers.
If opening debit and credit balances do not total the same, TallyPrime shows a Difference in opening balances line in the Balance Sheet. It is a reminder that something is missing — find and fix it before you go further.
