1Closing and reversing entries

Closing entries transfer every nominal (income and expense) account balance to the Trading and P&L account, which nets to profit, itself transferred to Capital (or retained earnings). Real and personal accounts are carried forward. Reversing entries on the first day of the next period undo accruals and prepayments, so routine payments next period can be booked normally without double-counting (optional, but common in manual and some ERP workflows).

2Provisions, contingent liabilities (Ind AS 37)

SituationTreatment
Present obligation, outflow probable (>50%), reliable estimateRecognise a provision at best estimate (discounted if material)
Possible obligation, or outflow not probable, or not measurableDisclose as contingent liability (unless remote)
Possible asset, inflow probableDisclose contingent asset; recognise only when virtually certain

Typical provisions: warranties, legal claims, onerous contracts, restoration. Restructuring provisions require a detailed formal plan and a valid expectation in those affected. "Provision for doubtful debts" is a valuation allowance, not an Ind AS 37 provision.

3Expected credit loss: provision matrix (Ind AS 109)

For trade receivables without a significant financing component, Ind AS 109 requires the simplified approach: lifetime expected credit losses from day one, often via a matrix of historical loss rates by ageing bucket, adjusted for forward-looking information.
