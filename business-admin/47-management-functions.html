3Span of control and layers

Span of control = how many people report to one manager. Wider spans mean fewer layers, faster communication and lower cost, but less coaching time.

How many layers?

Delegation passes authority to decide, while the manager keeps accountability. Good delegation matches authority to responsibility, sets clear outcomes and check-points, and develops people (situational leadership, Ch. 10).

3Design trade-offs

Every structure trades specialisation (functional depth, scale) against integration (coordination, speed to market). Galbraith's star model aligns strategy, structure, processes, rewards and people. Matrix structures add integration but violate unity of command and raise conflict; they work only with strong integrating processes. Holacracy and other "flat" experiments show that removing managers shifts coordination costs elsewhere rather than eliminating them.
