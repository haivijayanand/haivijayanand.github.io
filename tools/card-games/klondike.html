How to play Klondike

Goal: build all four foundations (top right) up by suit from Ace to King.

  • Tableau (7 columns): build down in alternating colours (red 9 on black 10). Move any face-up run as a unit. Only a King (or a run starting with a King) may go into an empty column. A face-down card is turned over automatically when uncovered.
  • Stock (top left): tap it to deal cards to the waste. Only the top waste card is playable. When the stock is empty, tap the empty spot to turn the waste back over.
  • Cards on a foundation may be moved back to the tableau if needed.
  • Tap a card: it goes to the best legal place — foundation first, otherwise a tableau column. Or drag it. Undo is unlimited. When everything is face up and the stock is empty, the game auto-finishes.

How many deals can be won?

Published solver studies estimate that roughly 79–82 % of Klondike deals are winnable with perfect play when the player may see every card ("thoughtful solitaire", draw-1). For ordinary play, where face-down cards are hidden, a strong human or program wins far less often — commonly quoted figures are about 35–45 % for draw-1 and lower for draw-3. These are estimates from published studies, not exact values; a small fraction of deals (around 0.25 %) cannot even make a first move.

1 · Where is a card I can't see?

At the deal there are 24 cards in the stock, 21 face-down in the tableau and 7 face-up. A card you cannot see is equally likely to be at any hidden position, so

P(in stock) = S ÷ (S + D), P(face-down) = D ÷ (S + D).

2 · Is at least one of my target cards in the stock?

If K wanted cards (e.g. the 4 Aces) are spread at random over N hidden positions and S of those positions are the stock, the number in the stock is hypergeometric:

P(X = j) = C(K, j) · C(N − K, S − j) ÷ C(N, S), and P(X ≥ 1) = 1 − C(N − K, S) ÷ C(N, S).

3 · Draw-3: which stock cards can I reach?

With draw-3 you only see every third card on a pass: positions 3, 6, 9 … from the top, plus the last card when the stock size is not a multiple of 3. Playing a card from the waste shifts everything after it by one, which is how other cards become reachable on the next pass. Reachable on one untouched pass: ⌊n ÷ 3⌋ + (1 if n mod 3 ≠ 0).

Strategy tips

  • Prefer moves that turn over a face-down card; work on the longest face-down columns first.
  • Don't empty a column unless you have a King ready to fill it.
  • Don't rush every card to the foundation — low cards of the opposite colour may still be needed to hold tableau runs.
  • In draw-3, playing one waste card changes which cards are reachable on the next pass; think before you recycle.
  • When choosing which of two black 7s to move a red 6 onto, take the one that uncovers more.
