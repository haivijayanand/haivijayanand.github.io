Journal, Ledger and Trial Balance

How entries travel: from the journal (the day-by-day record) to ledger accounts (one page per account) to the trial balance (a list of every balance). Watch eight Avani transactions post themselves into T-accounts, step by step.

⏱ about 2 hours🧪 Lab: Posting to T-accounts❓ 10-question quiz

1Today's goals

  • Write a journal entry with date, Dr, Cr and narration
  • Post a journal entry to ledger (T) accounts
  • Balance a ledger account and say whether it is Dr or Cr
  • Prepare a trial balance and know what it does and does not prove
  • Relate these to TallyPrime's Day Book, Ledger and Trial Balance reports

2The journal

The journal is the book of first entry: transactions in date order, each with the account debited, the account credited (written with "To"), the amounts and a short explanation called the narration. In TallyPrime the Day Book report shows exactly this list — every voucher of a day or period.

01-04-2026  SBI Current A/c ........ Dr 50,00,000
            To Share Capital ............... 50,00,000
            (Being share capital brought in by the promoters)

3The ledger and the T-account

A ledger account collects every entry that touches one account. Drawn as a "T": debits on the left, credits on the right. Debit-side entries are written "To other account", credit-side entries "By other account". The difference between the two sides is the balance; if debits are bigger it is a debit balance.

In TallyPrime every ledger is a T-account kept for you: open any ledger (Alt+G → Ledger) and you see its entries and the running balance. You never post by hand — accepting a voucher posts it to every ledger it names.

4The trial balance

List every ledger with its closing balance in a Dr or Cr column. Because each entry had equal debits and credits, the two columns must total the same. TallyPrime always shows a Trial Balance that agrees — it refuses unbalanced vouchers.

What a trial balance does not catch: a transaction left out entirely; the wrong amount on both sides; a debit to the wrong account of the same kind (Rent instead of Repairs); a full entry reversed. These are why TallyPrime gives you drill-down, bank reconciliation, ledger checks and the edit log.

5Lab: post the journal

Eight entries from Avani's first days. Press S to post one at a time and watch the T-accounts grow; when all are posted, the trial balance appears.

6In TallyPrime (look, don't touch yet)

  1. Day Book: Gateway → Day Book — the journal, voucher by voucher.
  2. A ledger: Alt+G → Ledger → pick one — the T-account as a list with a running balance.
  3. Trial Balance: Gateway → Trial Balance; press Enter on any line to drill down to its ledger, and again to a voucher.
