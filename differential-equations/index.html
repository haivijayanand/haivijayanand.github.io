15-Day Differential Equations Course

A course for engineers and curious minds who want to see differential equations, not just solve them. Each day starts from a real process, builds the equation from the rates, solves it where it can be solved, reads its behaviour where it cannot, and then lets you play: every lab draws the equation itself — its slope field, phase portrait, poles or spectrum — next to the solution and its derivatives, live as you move the sliders. Optional "Deeper" boxes carry the proofs for those who want them. Day 15 ends with a final assessment and a certificate.

How each day works1 Meet the process and its rates · 2 Build the equation · 3 Solve it, or read its behaviour · 4 Follow the worked example · 5 Play with the lab and watch the solution and its derivative move · 6 Try the practice problems · 7 Take the quiz and mark the day complete.
What you needCalculus at Class XII or first-year engineering level: derivatives, integrals, ex, sin and cos, and a little matrix algebra (it is revised on Day 8). Complex numbers appear from Day 5 and are explained where they come in. Boyce & DiPrima, Strogatz (Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos) and Kreyszig are good companions.
KeyboardM contents · F full screen · T light/dark · P play · S step · X pause · [ ] previous / next day.
How to read the plotsBlue solid = the solution, orange = its derivative or a second quantity, dashed = an exact answer or a reference. In slope fields and phase planes, click anywhere to start a new solution from that point. Colour maps run dark blue (cold / low) to orange (hot / high).
