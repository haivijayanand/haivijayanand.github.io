GST Returns, Reverse Charge, ITC Set-off, E-invoice and E-way Bill

From invoices to returns: GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B, the Invoice Management System (IMS) for supplier invoices, how input tax credit is set off against output tax, reverse-charge entries, e-invoice (IRN) and e-way bill — and what applies to a company of Avani's size.

1Today's goals

  • Know what GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B contain and when they are due
  • Set off ITC in the legal order
  • Book a reverse-charge expense
  • Know when e-invoicing and e-way bills apply
  • File-ready checks in TallyPrime

2The monthly cycle

Small taxpayers can opt for quarterly filing (QRMP). Dates can be extended by notification — always check the portal.

3Set-off of input tax credit

ITC is used in a fixed order: IGST credit first against IGST, then against CGST and SGST; CGST credit against CGST, then IGST; SGST credit against SGST, then IGST. CGST can never pay SGST, nor SGST pay CGST. Whatever is left is paid in cash.

4Reverse charge, e-invoice, e-way bill

  • RCM entry: book the expense (for example GTA freight ₹20,000) normally; TallyPrime raises the RCM liability (5 % for GTA in this example) in the GST reports when the ledger is marked "reverse charge applicable"; pay it in cash; claim it as ITC in the same return.
  • E-invoice: B2B invoices must be registered on the Invoice Registration Portal (IRN and QR code) once aggregate turnover in any year since 2017-18 crosses ₹5 crore. Avani's ₹2.74 crore turnover is below it today — but the day it crosses, TallyPrime can generate the IRN from the invoice itself.
  • E-way bill: needed to move goods of value above ₹50,000 in one consignment (and for some job-work movements whatever the value). TallyPrime generates it online from the invoice or delivery note with vehicle details.

5Now in TallyPrime

  1. Open GSTR-1: check "Uncertain transactions" (missing GSTIN, HSN or rate) and fix them first.
  2. Open GSTR-3B: compare ITC in books with GSTR-2B / IMS; investigate any supplier invoice you booked that the supplier has not uploaded.
  3. Record the tax payment with a Payment voucher (Dr Output CGST / SGST / IGST, Cr SBI) after set-off; TallyPrime's Stat Payment button (Alt+S in a payment) fills the amounts.
  4. Upload or file from TallyPrime if your release and connection allow; otherwise export JSON and upload on the GST portal.
