Job Work, Stock Transfers and Physical Stock

The rest of factory stock movement: sending gears out for heat treatment (job work with Material Out and Material In), moving stock between godowns with a Stock Journal, and correcting book stock to the counted stock with Physical Stock.

⏱ about 2 hours🧪 Lab: Practice Desk: inventory vouchers❓ 10-question quiz

1Today's goals

  • Send material to a job worker and receive it back
  • Book the job worker's bill
  • Transfer stock between godowns
  • Record a physical stock count
  • Understand why job work matters for GST (ITC-04)

2Job work: heat treatment at Heat-Tech Treaters

Avani machines gear blanks but does not own a furnace. Blanks go to Heat-Tech Treaters, come back hardened, and Heat-Tech bills only its processing charge. The steel never belongs to Heat-Tech — it is Avani's stock lying with a job worker.

Turn on F11 → Inventory → Enable job order processing. TallyPrime then tracks stock with each job worker and builds the ITC-04 data (goods sent to and received from job workers) required under GST.

3Godown transfers and physical stock

Stock Journal (Alt+F7) moves stock from one godown to another — the same item appears in source and destination with different godowns. Physical Stock (Ctrl+F7) records the count; TallyPrime adjusts the book quantity to it and the difference shows as a shortage or excess in stock reports.

4Practice Desk: inventory vouchers

5Now in TallyPrime

  1. F11: enable job order processing. Create a godown Heat-Tech Treaters (or let TallyPrime create the job worker's location).
  2. Enter the five vouchers. Material Out / Material In are under F10 (all vouchers).
  3. Reports: Job Work Out Order Summary, Godown Summary, and Stock Summary (Ctrl+F7 shortages show as physical stock differences).
