What should you do to your partner?

3 Dec 20233 min readRelationship

Impressing a relation

It’s important to note that every person is unique, so what might impress one man/woman might not impress another. It’s crucial to be genuine and respectful in your approach. Here are some typical ways that might help you (as a male) to impress a female friend / lover / partner and potentially lead to a romantic relationship:

1. Show Genuine Interest:

  • Listen Actively: Pay attention when she talks, and show that you are interested in what she has to say.
  • Ask Questions: Encourage her to share more about herself by asking thoughtful questions.

2. Be Confident:

  • Positive Body Language: Maintain eye contact, smile, and stand tall.
  • Speak Clearly: Communicate your thoughts and feelings confidently.

3. Be Kind and Respectful:

  • Show Respect: Respect her boundaries and personal space.
  • Kind Gestures: Small acts of kindness can leave a lasting impression.

4. Have a Sense of Humor:

  • Be Playful: A good sense of humor can be very attractive.
  • Laugh Together: Share funny stories or jokes to lighten the mood.

5. Be Supportive:

  • Encourage Her: Support her goals and ambitions.
  • Be There for Her: Offer a helping hand or a listening ear when she needs it.

6. Show Your Intelligence:

  • Engage in Stimulating Conversations: Talk about interesting topics.
  • Share Your Knowledge: But be careful not to come off as know-it-all.

7. Take Care of Your Appearance:

  • Dress Well: Wear clean, well-fitted clothes.
  • Personal Hygiene: Pay attention to your grooming.

8. Be Authentic:

  • Be Yourself: Don’t try to be someone you’re not.
  • Show Vulnerability: It’s okay to open up and show your true feelings.

9. Share Common Interests:

  • Find Common Ground: Engage in activities that you both enjoy.
  • Explore New Interests Together: Be open to trying out new things with her.

10. Be Romantic:

  • Thoughtful Gestures: Surprise her with small gifts or handwritten notes.
  • Plan Special Dates: Organize dates that show you’ve put thought into them.

11. Show Stability:

  • Be Reliable: Keep your promises and be someone she can count on.
  • Show Maturity: Handle situations calmly and maturely.

12. Be Positive and Upbeat:

  • Maintain a Positive Attitude: Try to stay upbeat and positive in your interactions.
  • Be Encouraging: Encourage a positive outlook on life.

13. Show Empathy and Understanding:

  • Be Compassionate: Show understanding and compassion when she’s going through a tough time.
  • Validate Her Feelings: Acknowledge her feelings and show that you understand.

14. Have Ambitions and Goals:

  • Share Your Aspirations: Talk about your goals and what you are passionate about.
  • Work Toward Your Goals: Show that you are motivated and working towards something in life.

15. Be Fun to Be Around:

  • Be Adventurous: Be willing to try new things and have fun together.
  • Create Positive Experiences: Make sure your time together is enjoyable and memorable.

In Conclusion, building a romantic relationship requires time, effort, and mutual interest. Be patient, be yourself, and focus on building a strong connection based on trust and respect. Remember, the key is to be genuine in your efforts and to respect her feelings and boundaries at all times.
