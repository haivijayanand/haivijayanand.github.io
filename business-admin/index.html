Business Administration

How a business works,
in three levels

Fifty chapters, from “what is a business?” to reading a balance sheet and judging a company by its ratios. Each chapter explains the same idea three ways on one page. Every chapter has step-by-step animations, sorting exercises, flip cards, calculators and quizzes.

🎒 Level 1 · School

Plain words and a lemonade-stall or kirana-shop story. A moving picture you control with P, S and X, and a short quiz.

💼 Level 2 · Working people

How the idea works in a real Indian business, with live calculators, worked examples and practice exercises.

🎓 Level 3 · Experts

A compact review: standards (Ind AS, Companies Act), formulas, models, edge cases and debates.
