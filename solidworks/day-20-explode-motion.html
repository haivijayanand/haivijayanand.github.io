28-Day SolidWorks Course · Week 3 · Day 20

Interference, Exploded Views and Motion Studies

Check that it fits, show how it goes together, and see it move: interference detection, exploded views and motion studies.

⏱ about 2–3 hours🧪 Lab: 3D exploded view❓ 5-question quiz

1Today's goals

  • Run Interference Detection and Clearance Verification
  • Create exploded views with explode lines
  • Make Animation and Basic Motion studies
  • Know what Motion Analysis adds

2Evaluate, explode, move

ToolWhereUse
Interference DetectionEvaluate tabFinds overlapping volumes. Options: treat coincidence as interference, ignore hidden bodies, include multibody parts, create fasteners and matching cosmetic-thread folders
Clearance VerificationEvaluate tabLists gaps smaller than a value you set
Hole AlignmentEvaluate tabFinds holes that don't line up across parts
Collision DetectionMove Component ▸ Collision DetectionStops a dragged part at contact
Exploded ViewAssembly tab / ConfigurationManagerSteps that move parts apart; regular and radial steps; explode lines

Motion studies (tab at the bottom)

AnimationKeyframes and the Animation Wizard (rotate model, explode/collapse). Motors drive at constant speed. No physics.
Basic MotionAdds gravity, springs, contact and motors with approximate physics. Good for presentations.
Motion AnalysisNeeds the SOLIDWORKS Motion add-in (Premium): forces, torques, velocities, accelerations, plots, results exported to Simulation.

3Do it in SolidWorks: bearing housing

  1. Assemble housing, bearing 6204, shaft, cover and 4 cap screws (or open your gearbox).
  2. Evaluate ▸ Interference Detection ▸ Calculate. Expect the bearing/housing press fit and the screw threads; tick Create fasteners folder and Create matching cosmetic threads folder so those are grouped apart from real problems.
  3. Assembly ▸ Exploded View: select the cover, drag the triad arrow up 120 → Done. Then the screws 190, the bearing 70, the shaft down 110.
  4. Add Explode Line Sketch (Smart Lines) to show paths.
  5. Right-click the exploded view in the ConfigurationManager ▸ Animate collapse.
  6. Motion Study 1 ▸ Animation Wizard ▸ Collapse, 4 s. Add a Rotary Motor on the shaft at 60 rpm. Calculate and play; save as AVI/MP4.

4Lab: exploded view

Drag the explode slider or press P to animate explode/collapse, S to step in quarters. Rotate the view while it runs.

5Pro tips and common mistakes

  • Every interference must be explained: press fit (intended), thread (cosmetic), or a design error. Record intended ones in a note.
  • Exploded views belong to a configuration — make them in the configuration you will draw.
  • Explode steps in the order the fitter would remove parts — your view becomes the assembly instruction.
  • Animation ignores physics; if parts pass through each other in a Basic Motion study, add Contact.

6Practice

Explode the Day 16 vice in assembly order, add explode lines, make a 6-second collapse animation and a screw-turning animation (rotary motor on the screw + screw mate) and export both videos.
