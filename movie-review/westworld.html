Westworld is set in the 2050s in a Wild West theme park run by the company Delos. Wealthy guests pay to live out any fantasy — heroism, violence, desire — with “Hosts”, lifelike androids programmed never to harm a human. Each night the Hosts are repaired and their memories wiped, and the story loop starts again.

Created by Jonathan Nolan (co-writer of The Dark Knight and Interstellar) and Lisa Joy, it is based on Michael Crichton’s 1973 film — listed in our robot & AI film timeline — and loosely on its 1976 sequel Futureworld. Over four seasons it moves from the park to the real world, where an AI called Rehoboam quietly controls human lives.

The 1973 film asked what happens when the robots break. The series asks what happens when they wake up — and whether we are any freer than they are.

Season 1 · The Maze

2016 · 10 episodes

Dolores, a rancher’s daughter, lives the same sunny day again and again; Maeve runs the saloon; a mysterious Man in Black hunts for a hidden “maze”. Park founder Dr. Robert Ford releases a small update called “Reveries” that lets Hosts recall fragments of old memories — and cracks start to appear.

Dolores and Maeve begin to wake. Bernard, Ford’s head of programming, learns he is himself a Host built in the image of Arnold, Ford’s dead co-founder. The Man in Black is revealed to be the older William, and the story has been running on two timelines thirty years apart. In the finale, Dolores shoots Ford — as he intended.

Why it worked

  • A mystery box with a payoff — the two-timeline twist is earned.
  • Anthony Hopkins as Ford, god of a small world.
  • HBO’s most-watched first season until The Last of Us (about 12 million cumulative viewers).

Season 2 · The Door

2018 · 10 episodes

The Hosts revolt. Dolores leads an armed uprising across the parks; Maeve searches for the daughter she remembers from a previous story loop; Bernard’s scrambled memories make time itself unreliable.

Dolores escapes the park carrying stolen Host cores. Maeve helps other Hosts cross into the Sublime, a digital world where they can live free. The Man in Black kills his own daughter Emily, convinced she is a Host — the season’s cruellest moment.

Verdict in brief

  • Bigger, bloodier and still well reviewed (86%).
  • The shuffled timelines start to feel like a puzzle for its own sake.
  • Thandiwe Newton won the Emmy for Outstanding Supporting Actress that year.

Season 3 · The New World

2020 · 8 episodes

Outside the park at last, Dolores enters a sleek future Los Angeles. She recruits Caleb (Aaron Paul), a veteran with no prospects, against Engerraund Serac (Vincent Cassel) and Rehoboam — an AI that predicts and steers every human life.

Dolores sacrifices herself after exposing what Rehoboam really does, and the revelation tips humanity into civil war. The Hosts’ question — am I free? — is turned on the humans.

Verdict in brief

  • A bold change into near-future thriller.
  • Loses the Western texture that made the show distinctive.
  • Viewership halved from season 2.

Season 4 · The Choice

2022 · 8 episodes

Years later, a Host copy of Charlotte Hale rules a world where humans have been made docile by an engineered virus and controlled by “tone” signals. Christina, a video-game writer played by Evan Rachel Wood, starts to suspect that her ordinary life is a story someone else is writing.

Christina learns her reality is manufactured. William disrupts the tone transmissions, and the season ends with Dolores proposing “one last game” — a setup for a fifth season that was never made.

Verdict in brief

  • A partial return to form, with a haunting final image.
  • The most expensive season ($160M+) and the least watched.
  • Ends on a cliffhanger after the show’s cancellation.

Music

Ramin Djawadi — also the composer of Game of Thrones — wrote the score, built around a restless piano-and-strings theme. His most famous trick is the player piano in the saloon, which plays old-time arrangements of modern songs. Hearing a rock song come out of a 19th-century piano tells the audience, without a word, that this “Old West” is fake.

Each licensed song cost between $15,000 and $55,000 to use.

Technology & craft

Shot on film

In the age of digital television, Westworld was shot on 35 mm Kodak film and processed by FotoKem — giving the desert its warm, grainy, cinema look.

Real Western landscapes

The park’s mesas and canyons were filmed in Utah, including Castle Valley and Dead Horse Point State Park; interiors and the town were built on California soundstages and backlots.

The Host lab

The white workshops where Hosts are printed, repaired and questioned became the show’s visual signature — clinical, glass-walled spaces set against the dusty park.

A theory of mind

The story borrows Julian Jaynes’s “bicameral mind” idea — that early humans heard their own thoughts as the voice of gods. The Hosts become conscious when they realise the voice in their head is their own.

“These violent delights…”

A line from Romeo and Juliet, “These violent delights have violent ends”, works inside the story as a trigger that spreads the awakening from Host to Host.

Filming through COVID

Season 4 production was briefly halted in 2021 after a COVID-19 case on set.

Emotional arc across the series

Intensity (0–10) at key moments, scored for this review. Dashed lines separate the four seasons.

Where the emotions come from

Dolores’s morning. The same cheerful line, the same dropped can, every day. Once you know her memories are wiped each night, the routine becomes heartbreaking.
Maeve’s daughter. A mother who remembers a child that was only ever a story — and chooses to save her anyway. Thandiwe Newton’s Emmy-winning arc is the show’s warmest thread.
Bernard’s discovery. A man finding out he was built, not born, is the season-one twist that hits hardest.
William’s fall. The Man in Black killing his own daughter because he can no longer tell human from Host is the show’s darkest moment.
Rehoboam. Season three turns the theme outward: if an AI is predicting your life, are you so different from a Host on a loop?
One last game. The series ends not with an answer but with an invitation — fitting for a story about who writes our lives.

How to watch

  • Must see: Season 1 — it works as a complete story.
  • Worth it: Season 2, for Maeve and the Man in Black.
  • For fans: Seasons 3 and 4, knowing the story is left unresolved.
  • Pair with: I, Robot and The Matrix — three takes on machines that refuse their programming.

Awards & fate

Awards

  • 9 Primetime Emmy wins from 54 nominations
  • Thandiwe Newton: Outstanding Supporting Actress in a Drama Series (2018)

Cancellation

  • HBO cancelled the show on 4 November 2022, while Nolan and Joy were planning a fifth and final season
  • The main cast were still paid for that season

Pulled from streaming

  • Removed from HBO Max in December 2022 as part of Warner Bros. Discovery cost-cutting, and offered to free ad-supported services instead — a rare fate for a flagship show
