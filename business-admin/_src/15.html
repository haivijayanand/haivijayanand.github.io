2CTC, take-home and the 50% wage rule

Under the Code on Wages (in force since 21 November 2025), "wages" for PF, gratuity and other benefits cannot be less than half of total remuneration: if allowances excluded from wages exceed 50%, the excess is added back. Many employers restructured salaries so basic + DA is at least 50% of pay, which raises PF and gratuity and can lower take-home.

3Appraisal biases and HR analytics

Common rating errors: halo/horn, leniency and central tendency, recency, similar-to-me, and contrast effects. Remedies: behaviourally anchored scales, calibration meetings, multiple raters, documented evidence. Forced distribution ("rank and yank") reduces leniency but harms collaboration; many firms moved to continuous feedback.

Annual attrition % = leavers in year ÷ average headcount × 100 Retention (cohort) = joiners still employed after 12 months ÷ joiners
