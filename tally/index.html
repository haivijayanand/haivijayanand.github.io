30-Day TallyPrime Course

Learn accounting and TallyPrime together, from your first transaction to the Balance Sheet. No accounting background is assumed: Week 1 teaches the ideas behind every entry, then each day adds one part of TallyPrime and practises it on the books of Avani Gearworks Pvt Ltd, a small gearbox manufacturer in Chennai — buying steel, castings and bearings, machining and assembling gearboxes, sending gears out for heat treatment, selling across states with GST, paying wages and closing the year. The Practice Desk on most days is a keyboard voucher simulator in your browser that checks every entry; each day also gives the same entries to make in real TallyPrime in its free Educational mode.

30 days · ≈ 2 h eachPractice Desk voucher simulator290 quiz questions + 20-question final examTallyPrime 7.x · GST · TDS · payroll

Choose the company whose books you will keep

Pick the kind of firm closest to yours. Every day's examples, Practice Desk entries, items, parties, bill of materials, GSTIN and state switch to it — the accounting and every rupee amount stay the same, so the course works identically for all of them. You can change it any time; your desk entries are kept separately for each company.

How each day works1 Read the idea in plain words · 2 See it in Avani Gearworks' books · 3 Follow the TallyPrime steps · 4 Practise in the lab or the Practice Desk · 5 Repeat the entries in your own TallyPrime · 6 Take the 10-question quiz and mark the day complete.
What you needA Windows PC with TallyPrime installed (free download; without a licence it runs in Educational mode, where vouchers can be dated only the 1st, 2nd or 31st of a month — every practice entry in this course uses those dates). Phones and tablets can do all the reading, labs and quizzes.
KeyboardM contents · F full screen · T light/dark · P play · S step · X pause · [ ] previous / next day. Inside the Practice Desk the TallyPrime keys work: F4–F9, Alt+F5…, Enter, Ctrl+A.
The running exampleAvani Gearworks Pvt Ltd (fictional), Ambattur, Chennai, Tamil Nadu — 40 people, CNC machining and assembly of the GB-20 gearbox and SG-40 gear sets. Suppliers in Chennai, Coimbatore and Pune; customers in Tamil Nadu, Telangana and Kerala, so you meet both CGST + SGST and IGST.
