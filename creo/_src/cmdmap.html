The five big differences. (1) Creo sketches are always fully defined — weak dimensions fill the gaps. (2) Creo saves numbered versions and keeps models in session memory: set the working directory, erase what you don't need. (3) Names are unique in session; assemblies find parts by name. (4) Datum planes and axes are central — build on them. (5) Regeneration failures stop you until you resolve them; fragile references cost more in Creo.
