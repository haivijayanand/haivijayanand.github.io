SolidWorks Course Part II · Week 6 · Day 38

Engineering Reports with AI Help

Turn model data into a design report people trust. Collect the facts from SolidWorks, let an AI assistant draft the words, and check every number against the source.

⏱ about 2–3 hours🧪 Lab: Number checker❓ 5-question quiz

1Today's goals

  • Structure an engineering design report
  • Extract facts from Mass Properties, Simulation and Interference
  • Prompt an AI assistant to draft from facts only
  • Verify numbers, units and derived values

2Facts first, words second

A design report has a predictable structure: purpose and scope → design inputs and requirements → description of the design → analysis and checks → results against requirements → conclusions and open points → references and appendices. Your organisation's format (for example a DRDO or ISO 9001 template) comes first.

The engineer's job is the facts: requirements, results, assumptions. An AI assistant is good at the words: structure, consistent tense, clear sentences, tables. It is not a source of engineering values — it can mistype a decimal, round, swap units or "fill in" a factor of safety it computed itself.

Use only AI tools your organisation approves for the data involved. For classified or export-controlled projects, the facts may not leave the approved network at all — then use the template structure, not the tool.

3Do it

  1. Collect the facts for the reducer housing into a plain list: key = value with units and source.
  2. Write the report skeleton (headings) in your company template.
  3. Prompt your approved AI assistant with the template above, one section at a time.
  4. Paste the draft into the lab's number checker with your fact list. Fix every flagged number.
  5. Recompute any derived value yourself (factor of safety, percentages) and state the formula.
  6. Add figures with captions and the source file name and revision.
  7. Review: a second engineer checks numbers against the source, not against the draft.

4Lab: number checker

Every number in the AI draft is compared with the source list. Matches are underlined; numbers that are a ratio of two source values are marked for you to recompute; anything else is flagged. Try editing the draft.

5Pro tips and common mistakes

  • Keep the fact list in the report's appendix — reviewers can trace every number.
  • Ask for one section at a time; long one-shot reports drift.
  • Watch units in particular: N vs kN, MPa vs N/mm² (same), mm vs m.
  • The conclusion — acceptable or not — is an engineering judgement. Write it yourself.
