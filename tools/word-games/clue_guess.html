    How Clue Guess works

    Each round has a set of words. For every word you see a riddle-style clue and a row of empty boxes, one per letter. Type your guess and press Check. If you are stuck, buy an easier clue or reveal letters, but each help lowers what the word is worth.

    The clue ladder

    1. Riddle. An indirect clue, always shown. For example, for CAMEL: "The ship of the desert".
    2. Meaning. A plain definition, for example "Desert animal with humps". Free on Easy; costs points on Medium and Hard.
    3. Letters. Each reveal fills one box: the first letter first, then random ones. At least one letter always stays hidden.
    4. Skip. Shows the answer and scores nothing for that word.

    Once a word is solved or skipped, both clues and the answer stay on screen so the word is learnt either way.

    Streak bonus

    Solving words in a row builds a streak. The second word in a streak earns +5, the third +10, the fourth +15, and so on. A skip resets the streak to zero.

    Where the clues come from

    The same 319 themed words as Word Search: 159 for Kids and 160 for Adult, in English and Science themes. Each word has a meaning and a separate riddle written to point at the word without naming it. Words don't repeat within a session until every word in your chosen pool has been played.
