30-Day Manufacturing Course

How a factory really works — and how to make it work better. Written for engineers who run lines and managers who run plants, the course moves from the factory as a system to planning, quality, lean, maintenance, safety, people, energy, suppliers, sustainability, data, costing and investment. Every day explains the idea, gives the formula or method, shows it on a flexible packaging plant (extrusion, printing, lamination, slitting and bag making) and on other industries, works an example, and lets you experiment in a live lab. Day 30 is a plant-improvement capstone, a final assessment and a certificate.

Each day also gives examples from machining, assembly, food and process plants, so the method carries over to any factory.
