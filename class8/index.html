How to use these pages

  1. Open a subject and pick a chapter from the side list.
  2. Read the key idea, then play the animation one step at a time.
  3. Try the lab, then do the practice questions; each set is fresh.
  4. Finish a chapter's test to earn its certificate.
  5. To use offline, open the page once while online and keep the tab, or save it with Ctrl+S.

More school pages (Fraction Board, Grid Paths Explorer) are in the School education group on the Tools page.
