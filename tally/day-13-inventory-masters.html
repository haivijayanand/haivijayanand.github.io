Inventory Masters: Stock Groups, Units, Godowns and Items

Switch on inventory and build the masters a manufacturer needs: stock groups (raw material to finished goods), units of measure including compound units, godowns (stores, shop floor, finished goods), and stock items with HSN, GST rate and opening stock.

⏱ about 2 hours🧪 Lab: Stock group sorter + item creation❓ 10-question quiz

1Today's goals

  • Integrate accounts with inventory and know what changes
  • Design stock groups for a manufacturer
  • Create simple and compound units
  • Create godowns for stores, shop floor and finished goods
  • Create a stock item with its opening quantity, rate and godown

2Inventory switched on

With F11 → Inventory → Maintain inventory = Yes and Integrate accounts with inventory = Yes, every purchase and sale moves quantities as well as rupees, the Stock Summary shows what you hold, and the closing stock value flows straight into the Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet. Turn on Multiple godowns too.

3The four inventory masters

MasterPurposeAvani's choice
Stock GroupFamilies of items, for reports and common settingsRaw Materials · Bought-out Components · Consumables · Work in Progress · Finished Goods · Scrap
Unit of MeasureSimple (kg, nos, ltr, set) or compound (Box of 10 nos)kg for steel and scrap, nos for castings, bearings and gearboxes, ltr for oil, set for gear sets, "Box of 10 nos" for inserts
Godown (location)Where stock physically sitsMain Stores · Shop Floor · Finished Goods Store · stock lying with Heat-Tech Treaters (job worker)
Stock ItemThe thing itselfEN19 Steel Bar Dia 60, Housing Casting GB-20, Bearing 6208, Oil Seal 40x62, Gear Oil 220, Gearbox GB-20, Spur Gear Set SG-40, MS Turnings Scrap…

Stock categories are an optional second way of grouping (for example by size or customer) that cuts across groups.
