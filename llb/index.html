B.Com. LL.B. (Hons.) – study explorers

Interactive pages for each course of the five-year integrated B.Com. LL.B. (Hons.) programme, built for a first-year student at Tamil Nadu National Law University. Each page explains the syllabus unit by unit, with labs, step-through animations, flashcards, a quiz and references.

Every page runs in the browser and keeps working offline once opened. Progress is saved in your own browser only.

How to use these pages

  1. Open a course and read each unit's key idea first.
  2. Try the lab beside it, then answer the three check questions.
  3. Mark the unit done; your progress shows in the side list.
  4. Before exams, use the flashcards, quiz and practice pages.
  5. To use offline, open the page once while online and keep the tab, or save it with Ctrl+S.

Unit content is a reference syllabus based on standard Indian law-university and B.Com. syllabi. Compare it with the official course outline issued by the university.
