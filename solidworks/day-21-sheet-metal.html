28-Day SolidWorks Course · Week 3 · Day 21

Sheet Metal: Flanges, Bends and Flat Patterns

Brackets, enclosures, chutes and guards are bent from flat sheet. SolidWorks models them folded and unfolds them exactly — if you understand K-factor and bend allowance.

⏱ about 2–3 hours🧪 Lab: Flat length calculator❓ 5-question quiz

1Today's goals

  • Create Base Flange, Edge Flange, Miter Flange, Hem, Jog, Sketched Bend
  • Set thickness, bend radius and K-factor (or gauge tables)
  • Flatten and make a flat-pattern drawing
  • Calculate bend allowance, setback and bend deduction

2Sheet metal logic

The first sheet-metal feature (Base Flange/Tab) creates three tree items: Sheet-Metal (default thickness, bend radius, bend allowance, relief), the flange, and Flat-Pattern (suppressed until you flatten). Everything after knows it is sheet.

FeatureUse
Base Flange/TabFirst wall from a closed or open sketch
Edge FlangeWall on an edge, with length to outer/inner virtual sharp or tangent
Miter FlangeFlanges along several edges with mitred corners
HemFolded-back edge (closed, open, teardrop, rolled)
JogZ-offset bend pair from a line
Sketched BendBend along a sketch line
Closed Corner / Corner ReliefClose gaps or add relief at corners
Forming ToolsLouvres, lances, dimples from the Design Library
Convert to Sheet Metal / Insert BendsTurn a shelled solid into sheet metal
Flat length. The neutral axis stretches neither way; it sits at K × T from the inside. BA = θ(R + K·T) (θ in radians). OSSB = tan(θ/2)(R + T). BD = 2·OSSB − BA. Flat = L1 + L2 − BD, with L1 and L2 measured to the outside virtual sharp. Typical K: 0.33 for tight bends to 0.5 for large radii; your press shop's tested value beats any table.

3Do it in SolidWorks: L-bracket and enclosure

  1. Right Plane: open sketch, a line 50 then 30 at 90° (an L). Sheet Metal ▸ Base Flange/Tab: Mid Plane 60, thickness 2, bend radius 2, K-factor 0.44.
  2. Flatten (Sheet Metal tab). Measure the flat length; compare with the lab.
  3. Unflatten. Add Hole Wizard holes on each flange.
  4. Enclosure: rectangle 200 × 120 → Base Flange (2 mm). Edge Flange on all four edges, 40 high, length to outer virtual sharp.
  5. Closed Corner on the four corners, gap 0.2.
  6. Add a louvre from Design Library ▸ forming tools ▸ louvers.
  7. Drawing (Day 22): View Palette ▸ Flat pattern view, with bend lines and bend notes.

4Lab: flat length calculator

Change thickness, radius, angle and K-factor and see BA, setback, BD and the flat pattern update. A 90° bend: OSSB = R + T.

5Pro tips and common mistakes

  • Use gauge tables (Excel) holding thickness, radius and K-factor per material so everyone uses shop values.
  • Holes too close to a bend distort: keep at least 2.5 T + R from the bend.
  • Model in one direction consistently (inside or outside dimensions) — drawings follow.
  • Flat pattern export: right-click Flat-Pattern ▸ Export to DXF/DWG for laser cutting.

6Practice

A motor mounting bracket from 3 mm mild steel, inside radius 3, K = 0.4: base 120 × 80 with a 90° flange 60 high and a slot pattern for a NEMA 23 motor. Compute the flat length by hand, then check with Flatten. Export a DXF.
