SolidWorks Course Part II · Week 5 · Day 33

Revision Logs and ECNs with AI Help

Every change needs a record. Turn a before/after comparison into a revision-table entry and an engineering change notice, with AI help for the wording and you in charge of the facts.

⏱ about 2–3 hours🧪 Lab: Revision log builder❓ 5-question quiz

1Today's goals

  • Write clear revision descriptions
  • Draft an ECN: reason, change, effect, disposition
  • Use an AI assistant for wording, not for facts
  • Check where-used before releasing

2What a good change record says

What changedEach item with the old and new value: "BASE THK 20 WAS → 25".
WhyThe problem or request, with a reference (test report, NCR, customer request).
What is affectedParts, drawings, assemblies (where-used), tooling, CNC programs, manuals.
What to do with stockUse as is, rework, scrap, use up first — and from which batch the change applies (effectivity).

In SolidWorks: Annotation ▸ Tables ▸ Revision Table on the drawing; Add Revision creates a row and can place revision symbols (triangles) next to the changed dimensions. With PDM, the revision letter comes from the workflow.

Where AI helps

Give the assistant the facts as a list and ask it to turn them into clear, consistent wording in your format. Do not ask it to "figure out what changed" from screenshots, and never let it fill gaps — tell it to list missing information as questions.

Rewrite the change below as an engineering change notice in this format:
ECN no. / Part and drawing / Rev from → to / Reason / Changes (numbered,
old → new) / Affected documents / Stock disposition / Effectivity.
Rules: copy every number, size, tolerance and material grade exactly;
do not add changes; do not guess reasons; list anything missing as
questions at the end.
FACTS:
[paste the before/after list, reason, ECN number, disposition]

3Do it in SolidWorks

  1. Open the Day 22 pillow-block drawing. Make the changes from the lab (base 25, FG 260, M12 counterbores).
  2. Annotation ▸ Tables ▸ Revision Table (if not present), then right-click ▸ Revisions ▸ Add Revision.
  3. Place revision symbols on the changed dimensions; fill the description from the lab output.
  4. Check where-used (PDM Where Used, or open the assemblies) and list them in the ECN.
  5. Use the prompt template to polish the ECN wording; compare every number with your list.
  6. Release through the workflow (Day 32) or archive the PDF with the ECN.

4Lab: revision log builder

Edit the "After" column. The revision row, ECN draft and the AI prompt update. Only items that actually changed are listed; unchanged items are recorded as checked.

5Pro tips and common mistakes

  • "Updated drawing" is not a revision description. Say what and where.
  • One ECN can cover several documents, but each document gets its own revision.
  • Do not reuse revision letters; do not "silently" fix a released drawing.
  • Keep the AI's text and your fact list side by side when checking — read numbers, not sentences.

6Practice

Write the ECN for changing the reducer's input bearing from 6205 to 6205-2RS (sealed): what else changes (housing seal groove, lubrication note, BOM)? Draft the facts, have an AI assistant word it, then check it line by line.
