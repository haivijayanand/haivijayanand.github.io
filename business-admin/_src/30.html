1Share capital: the layers

TermMeaning
Authorised capitalThe maximum the memorandum allows (e.g., 10 lakh shares of ₹10 = ₹1 crore)
Issued capitalShares offered to investors
Subscribed capitalShares actually taken up
Called-up capitalPart of face value asked for so far

Equity shares carry votes and the residual profit; preference shares get a fixed dividend and priority in repayment, usually without votes. Under Ind AS, redeemable preference shares are usually classified as financial liabilities, not equity.

2Issue shares at a premium

Share issue entries

Securities premium can be used only for purposes in s.52: issuing fully paid bonus shares, writing off preliminary and share-issue expenses, premium on redemption of preference shares or debentures, and buyback (s.68). It cannot be paid as dividend.

3Dividends and bonus shares

Dividend

Dividends are declared out of current or past profits (s.123), after depreciation. Interim dividends are declared by the board; final dividends by shareholders at the AGM on the board's recommendation. Companies deduct TDS at 10% on dividends paid to a resident individual above ₹10,000 in a year (from FY 2025-26).

1Deferred tax (Ind AS 12)

Deferred tax arises from temporary differences between the carrying amount of an asset or liability and its tax base. Faster tax depreciation creates a deferred tax liability (more tax later); provisions deductible only when paid (e.g., gratuity) create a deferred tax asset, recognised only to the extent future taxable profit is probable. Ind AS 12 uses the balance-sheet approach; AS 22 uses timing differences in profit.

2Capital maintenance: buybacks

Section 68: a company may buy back up to 25% of paid-up capital plus free reserves (board approval up to 10%), funded from free reserves, securities premium or proceeds of a different issue; post-buyback debt-to-equity must not exceed 2:1. A capital redemption reserve equal to the nominal value bought back from free reserves is created (s.69). Since 1 October 2024, buyback proceeds are taxed in shareholders' hands as deemed dividend (the cost of shares becomes a capital loss), replacing the earlier company-level buyback tax.

3Other equity (Ind AS Schedule III)

Statement of changes in equity columns: securities premium, capital redemption reserve, general reserve, retained earnings, OCI reserves (FVOCI equity instruments, cash-flow hedge reserve, remeasurement of defined benefit plans, foreign currency translation), share options outstanding (ESOP reserve, Ind AS 102), and money received against share warrants. Debit balance of profit and loss is shown as a negative within other equity, not as an asset.
